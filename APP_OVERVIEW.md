# Goal Hint — App Overview

**Reviewed 28 November 2026.**

## Purpose and users

Goal Hint helps adult football followers browse fixtures, understand predictions, assemble slips, and check outcomes. Members can obtain access through subscriptions, trials, referral days, or administrator grants. Administrators manage accounts, payments, access and reporting. The public experience is available in English. Predictions are estimates. Bet placement and stake handling are outside the product’s scope. [Content access](implementation-docs/subscriptions.md), [prediction engine](implementation-docs/prediction-engine.md), [public app policy](next-js/src/domain/public-policy.ts).

## Football, predictions and slips

| Feature | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Matches and Results | `/en/matches` is the homepage. It opens on Today and Pending with live fixtures first. Eligible browser documents stream the shell through direct and service-worker navigation. Once the list is accepted, the period summary starts loading before client scripts finish and updates in place. An eligible initial list or summary failure gets one in-page retry while the shell stays visible. The list counts and pages fixtures with published predictions, including access-locked previews. Search and filters cover teams, competitions, dates, times, markets, results, probabilities and odds where permitted. Guests can see newer verified period counts for the same cohort while the list refreshes on its usual schedule. **Show More** appends up to 50 fixtures. A window of up to 200 rows uses one stored snapshot so live changes can refresh and extend it together. Numbered URLs remain addressable. [Matches and display](implementation-docs/markets-and-display.md), [public feed projection](next-js/src/server/matches/public-feed.ts), [fixtures and live data](implementation-docs/fixtures-and-live-data.md). |
| Match Details | Fixture pages can open before a forecast is available. They bring together score and status, featured and additional predictions, eligible markets and bookmaker prices, statistics, events, lineups, and prediction history where data is available. Desktop uses a section sidebar and independently scrolling content. Compact layouts use horizontal tabs. Explicit archived revisions keep the original forecast evidence. [Match Details](implementation-docs/markets-and-display.md#match-details-on-desktop-and-mobile), [odds display](implementation-docs/odds-and-market-updates.md#delivery-to-matches-match-details-and-slips). |
| Predictions and performance | The Local Engine uses point-in-time regulation scores and qualified league and team samples. Trusted provider 1X2 and derived double-chance forecasts can fill gaps when fallback is enabled. Qualified regulation forecasts include match and team goals and extensions such as correct score in applicable views. Upcoming featured picks require an eligible exact quote. The normal publication cutoff is five minutes before kickoff. Sealed picks retain their revision and selection, and settlement uses verified scores for the applicable period. `/en/performance` reports outcomes for predictions, markets and published slips. [Prediction engine](implementation-docs/prediction-engine.md), [performance and market views](implementation-docs/markets-and-display.md). |
| Best Slips | `/en/best-slips` opens on today’s Pending slips in the 2.50–5.00 odds band. In-app navigation opens the requested controls before the verified archive read finishes. A manual retry shows the controls and pending state while the archive reloads in place. The archive supports Kampala dates and ranges, six odds bands, result, probability and match-count filters, and stable date-and-rank links. The publisher rechecks each half hour for the 03:00-to-03:00 operational window. Each new slip uses one verified bookmaker, meets a 30-minute kickoff buffer and follows the fixture allocation and kickoff-span rules. Published selections, prices and ranks are sealed. Original-cycle results update as the matches settle. [Best Slips and publication](implementation-docs/automatic-slips.md). |
| Slip Builder and sharing | `/en/slips` shows the selected criteria while access and any requested build load. It groups odds, probability, markets, kickoff window, match count and fixture-reuse settings. Signed-in members can keep up to ten presets, and generation requires feature access. Each generated leg needs a qualifying stored forecast and a matching exact quote. A build is a transient result, and **Show More** reveals the next 20 cards from that result. Eligible slips export as branded images and can be shared with stable references. [Slip Builder](implementation-docs/slip-builder.md), [slip sharing](next-js/docs/slip-sharing.md). |

Average pick probability is the mean of the leg probabilities. New Best Slips omit a whole-slip win estimate because the joint model is awaiting qualification. Archived publications can retain earlier approved estimates. [Slip probabilities and limits](implementation-docs/automatic-slips.md).

## Accounts, access and payments

- **Accounts and recovery.** Phone-and-password signup admits the member, issues a session and can grant an eligible trial. Login and Register can open while optional support settings load, and a failed identity check has a same-page retry. Phone ownership is recorded separately. Accounts can keep two active sessions and sign out other devices. For manual password recovery, an administrator verifies control of the registered number before issuing a 24-hour reset link. A successful reset revokes existing sessions. [Authentication and recovery](implementation-docs/authentication.md).
- **Content access.** Guests can browse fixtures, scores, completed predictions and Best Slips previews. Paid Access **Off** gives admitted members full feature access and pauses new purchases and auto-renewal. **On** checks for a current paid period, trial, referral redemption or grant. Unlock links preserve a valid return path to Matches, Best Slips or Builder. [Subscriptions and access](implementation-docs/subscriptions.md).
- **Checkout and wallet.** Eligible members can pay in UGX by manual MTN MoMo or Airtel Money, apply part or all of their available wallet credit, or use hosted Flutterwave Uganda Mobile Money for a full package when configured. Manual top-ups credit the wallet, and earned referral days use a separate access redemption. Manual evidence and approval workflows handle shortfalls, disputes, and receipt ownership. Verified allocations protect against duplicate grants and record wallet and access changes together. Existing payment requests remain reviewable while new sales are paused. [Payments](implementation-docs/payments.md), [wallet and referrals](implementation-docs/wallet-and-referrals.md).
- **Member workspace and community.** Accounts show subscriptions, payments, wallet, referrals, devices and activity. A private inbox covers payment decisions, access changes and referral earnings. Eligible phone-verified referrals can earn access days. Community offers configured WhatsApp destinations and records a visitor’s join confirmation. [Account workspace](implementation-docs/authentication.md#account-workspace-and-notices), [referral eligibility](implementation-docs/wallet-and-referrals.md#invitations-and-reward-eligibility), [Community](implementation-docs/authentication.md#community-invitations).
- **Administration and analytics.** The protected owner workspace covers Users, payment review, access grants, plans, password requests, settings and audit. Users supports filtering, account detail and eligible bulk actions, with retention rules for erasure and deletion. Its directory, plans and selected account keep their own data and recovery states. Password Requests loads its queue and selected request independently. The shared owner account can also open first-party traffic and conversion reports. [Users administration](implementation-docs/subscriptions.md#user-administration), [password recovery](implementation-docs/authentication.md), [owner workspace](implementation-docs/ui-implementation.md#application-shells-and-routes), [analytics](next-js/docs/analytics.md#dashboard).

## How it runs

- **Shared web application.** Next.js owns routes, APIs, authorization and product logic. Prisma uses MySQL in local development and MariaDB on the hosted web environments. Flutter hosts the configured website in an embedded view and provides native navigation, appearance, sharing and download integration on supported platforms. Both use the shared light and dark experience. Foreground work uses the header progress bar. Feed recovery retains compatible, authorized results and identifies stale counts. Document recovery distinguishes server failures, offline state, slow headers and render errors. [UI implementation](implementation-docs/ui-implementation.md), [connection recovery](next-js/docs/offline-startup.md).
- **Football ingestion.** Private production workers collect API-Football schedules, results, fixture details and odds under shared quota and publication rules. Public pages read stored projections. Visible Matches and Match Details views normally refresh every 20 seconds during active play or near kickoff and every 60 seconds in quiet periods. Bookmaker prices require an exact eligible observation and applicable display permission. Current pre-match, in-play, closing and recovered prices keep their own evidence clocks. [Fixtures and live data](implementation-docs/fixtures-and-live-data.md), [odds and market updates](implementation-docs/odds-and-market-updates.md).
- **Deployment.** The checked-in release tooling promotes the same checksummed web archive through staging and production and verifies the migration ledger and HTTPS smoke checks. Installers live in a persistent production catalog, and scheduled workers keep separate release controls. [Deployment and version control](implementation-docs/deployment-and-version-control.md).
- **Private monitoring.** Existing WHM/cPanel checks supervise Apache and MariaDB. Munin and sysstat collect host counters. The staging VPS timer samples HTTPS, database readiness and queue metrics in the private system journal every five minutes. [Monitoring and rollback](next-js/docs/deployment/vps-watch.md).

## Current boundaries

- **Live checkout.** Both hosts show Privacy and Terms effective 22 November 2026. Production Contact publishes email and WhatsApp support. New production purchases require Paid Access On, the published notices and an enabled support contact. Manual transfers require a usable receiving account. Live Flutterwave requires public Call or WhatsApp support, merchant readiness and the live provider and webhook configuration. The earlier hosted sandbox test on staging build `699b8d70` created a payment link, then received HTTP 403 from Flutterwave before payment controls appeared. A synthetic signed callback exercised the handler. Provider-delivered confirmation, receipt approval and access issuance remain unverified for that attempt, and successful live collection has separate qualification. [Privacy](https://goalhint.com/en/privacy), [Terms](https://goalhint.com/en/terms), [Contact](https://goalhint.com/en/contact), [payment verification limits](implementation-docs/payments.md#implementation-limits).
- **Hosted feed performance.** MariaDB uses a 4 GiB InnoDB buffer and Passenger distributes production requests across a bounded pool. A 28 November audit recorded intermittent 503s on Matches and Best Slips, and one production 30-day Matches API response took 26.85 seconds. The repository includes private host observation and repeatable staging HTTP measurements. [Hosted resource tuning](next-js/docs/reports/performance-reliability-2026-11-28.md#production-resource-contention), [VPS audit](next-js/docs/reports/vps-optimization-2026-11-28.md).
- **Native distribution and alerts.** Physical Android-device acceptance remains open. A local unsigned Windows x64 package opened the production site in WebView2. Public Windows distribution requires Authenticode signing. Apple distribution and Linux packaging and runtime acceptance remain pending. Administrator payment alerts over Web Push, Android FCM and Windows need the applicable credentials and device qualification. [Flutter release status](flutter/docs/releases.md), [alert verification](next-js/docs/payment-review-alerts.md).
- **Model qualification.** Provider coverage, access rules and evidence determine which forecasts, markets and odds appear. Broader period, player and statistical models and historical or prospective accuracy claims still require qualification. [Prediction gaps and limits](implementation-docs/prediction-engine.md#gaps-and-limits).
