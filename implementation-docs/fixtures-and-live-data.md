# Fixtures, match details and live data

The private football worker pulls schedules, scores, results and match observations from API-Football and writes verified data to MySQL or MariaDB. The Matches page, Match Details and their APIs read the stored projections. An open browser polls Goal Hint for those projections. A visit makes no API-Football request. [Worker entry point](../next-js/src/workers/live.ts), [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Matches reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-feed.ts), [Match Details reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-detail.ts).

## Fixture discovery and results

The following are worker targets implemented in source for coordinated mode. Dates are Kampala reporting dates. Quota, provider failures and incomplete responses can delay individual updates. The recorded production measurements below describe the prior release. [Worker schedule](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runner.ts), [Result policy](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-policy.ts).

| Work | Normal cadence | Fetch and update mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily schedule | 00:00 Kampala time (21:00 UTC), with catch-up for the latest eligible slot on startup | The selection job imports `/fixtures?date=YYYY-MM-DD&timezone=Africa/Kampala` across eight dates on standard coverage, including the extra day for the seven-day visitor window. The Free plan attempts two dates. With the extended pre-match odds configuration, the same lease covers seven further dates after selection and cutoff scheduling. When eligible, the extended odds horizon reuses verified same-slot date receipts and revisits failed or deferred dates the following hour. Collection passes through the shared provider-day quota and pacing limiter. The defaults are at most 10 launches per rolling second, 600 per rolling minute and 120,000 per verified provider day, subject to the actual account limits. The result poller keeps a database lease and durable retry times. Capacity pressure stretches active checks and slows enrichment. The worker reports provider-receipt-to-database latency and reattaches a stopped poller on a subsequent maintenance iteration. Outside coordinated mode, the existing account-sized cadence applies. [Capacity policy](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Result service](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-service.ts), [Quota runbook](../next-js/docs/quota-limiter.md).

`FootballFixture` holds the canonical kickoff, teams, competition and status. Import receipts and result observations retain provenance. Accepted changes update the stored result and can requeue settlement. Regulation, extra-time and penalty shootout scores are verified and stored separately where confirmed. Missing or contradictory evidence can leave a fixture pending with its provider status and next check preserved. [Database schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Result write path](../next-js/src/server/results/result-write.ts), [Public status freshness](../next-js/src/server/results/result-status-freshness.ts), [Settlement and visibility report](../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-publication-settlement-2026-11-20.md).

Result batches resume within their provider-day lease. A crash across the UTC quota reset can leave an older sealed pending batch for operator review. The new day's scheduled checks verify still-due results with fresh observations. [Result batch recovery](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-mysql-store.ts), [Poller binding](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

## Match observations and details

`GOAL_HINT_LIVE_DETAILS_ENABLED` defaults off. The separate detail poller requires an eligible paid plan with at least 7,500 usable requests per day, at least 60 requests per minute and the required authority. When eligible, it budgets up to 20% of the usable provider day for detail collection and covers fixtures with or without a prediction. [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Plan limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Detail quota](../next-js/src/server/live/live-detail-quota.ts).

| Observation | Coordinated cadence and source |
| --- | --- |
| Live events and team statistics | Target 60 seconds for detailed team statistics per eligible live fixture, with slower collection under quota pressure. The collector reuses compatible recent observations from the broad live sweep, then requests `/fixtures?ids=` in batches of up to 20 for remaining fixtures. Dedicated endpoints accept one fixture per call. Missing sections can trigger bounded concurrent fallbacks. |
| Lineups and player data | Covered upcoming fixtures become eligible for lineup checks 90 minutes before kickoff and can be retried every 15 minutes until confirmed. The collector reuses embedded lineups or player statistics where available. Verified coverage and a missing section can trigger `/fixtures/lineups` or `/fixtures/players`. Player statistics follow the live or final detail checks. |
| Final match details | The first check becomes due 15 minutes after the first observed final, and a later check is due at 24 hours. Missing eligible final details can be retried within the three-day collection window. |

The noncoordinated collector normally uses separate single-fixture statistics and events requests each minute while live and checks recent finals every 15 minutes. The owned local profile can batch details. [Detail timing policy](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-contract.ts), [Collector](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-service.ts), [Due fixture selection](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-mysql-store.ts), [Match detail runbook](../next-js/docs/live-match-details.md#central-collection-and-request-budget).

API-Football describes upstream updates of about 15 seconds for events and one minute for team statistics where coverage is available. Those updates reach stored Match Details through the worker checks above. [API-Football endpoint guide](https://www.api-football.com/news/post/how-to-get-started-with-api-football-the-complete-beginners-guide).

`LiveMatchSnapshot` keeps verified events and team statistics with section-specific retrieval and provider clocks. The stored occurrence is tied to the fixture, kickoff, teams and applicable prediction cycle. `FixtureFacts` retains eligible lineup, player and richer statistics data for downstream use. During live play, a section with a retrieval clock older than five minutes can appear stale while the previous values remain visible. An absent section can show waiting, unavailable or partial coverage. The Lineups and Players tabs load stored Match Insights evidence on demand under their own access rules. The polled live snapshot supplies the separate Events and Statistics panels. [Snapshot reader](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-read.ts), [Match facts store](../next-js/src/server/football/fixture-facts-store.ts), [Insights reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/insights-read.ts), [Details tabs](../next-js/src/components/match/details/touchline-tabs.tsx).

An optional coordinated context job runs hourly after verified discovery of the current day. It reuses fresh data and rotates bounded requests for standings, injuries, team statistics and other reference data. Its normal freshness targets include one hour for standings, four hours for injuries, 12 hours for team statistics and one day for active player-statistics data. [Context schedule and source windows](../next-js/src/server/football/current-day-refresh.ts).

## What the browser refreshes

- **Matches** opens on Today in the browsing time zone. The standalone `GET /api/matches` defaults to a 30-day range. The ordinary list counts and pages eligible published predictions, including locked cards. The separate offline fixture projection can include fixtures without forecasts. **Match Details** and `GET /api/matches/{id}` read the stored fixture, applicable forecast and eligible match observations. `GET /api/matches/{id}/insights?section=…` supplies stored insight sections as needed. Live events and statistics are read outside the immutable forecast cache. [Feed query](../next-js/src/domain/feed-query.ts), [Matches reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-feed.ts), [Detail service](../next-js/src/server/matches/detail-service.ts), [Insights API](../next-js/src/server/matches/insights-service.ts).
- **Open views** refresh every 20 seconds while live, while the applicable run is updating, or when a scheduled kickoff is within 30 minutes either side of now. Otherwise they refresh every 60 seconds. The browser interval, upstream updates and network delays all contribute to the time before a change appears. Hidden or offline views pause and resume on visibility or reconnection. Polling merges newer fixture observations with stable forecast data. Historical revision inspection retains its selected occurrence. [Refresh policy](../next-js/src/domain/live-refresh.ts), [Client refresh runbook](../next-js/docs/live-client-refresh.md).

### Match cards, results and details

- Matches uses Live first by default. Live fixtures sort by their verified phase and progress, followed by scheduled fixtures in ascending kickoff order and then other results. Outcome cards use the same eligible displayed selection on both layouts. Selecting an outcome refreshes the list, count and pagination while the confirmed full-period performance values remain visible. Date, competition, market and other cohort filters affect the full-period values. [Feed ordering](../next-js/src/server/matches/feed-read.ts), [Displayed-result membership](../next-js/src/server/matches/feed-result-filter.ts), [Performance presentation](../next-js/src/components/match/feed-performance-summary.tsx).
- The compact cards and desktop rows update score, status and clock in their existing cells. Running time advances for up to 119 seconds from the last verified observation and respects the known phase boundary. The display waits for a provider observation to enter added time or a new phase. It shows a paused phase label or **Live** when minutes are unavailable. Final rows can show verified extra-time and shootout scores. Regulation predictions grade from the regulation score, and a pick can remain pending while its required score evidence is missing. [Match card](../next-js/src/components/match/match-card.tsx), [Desktop row](../next-js/src/components/match/desktop-match-row.tsx), [Clock rules](../next-js/src/domain/match-clock.ts), [Settlement](../next-js/src/domain/market-settlement.ts).
- Match Details uses a desktop sidebar and compact horizontal tabs for the same information sections. The desktop **Match Details** section shows its scoreboard and Top Predictions. The compact Verdict remains above the tabs. Event and statistic panels use the stored live snapshot, and lineup and player sections can load their own stored evidence on demand. Section availability follows viewer access and verified coverage. [Desktop workspace](../next-js/src/components/match/details/desktop-match-workspace.tsx), [Shared detail sections](../next-js/src/components/match/details/touchline-tabs.tsx), [UI implementation](ui-implementation.md).
