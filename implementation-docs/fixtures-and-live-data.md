# Fixtures, match details and live data

The private football worker pulls schedules, scores, results and match observations from API-Football and writes verified data to MySQL or MariaDB. The Matches page, Match Details and their APIs read the stored projections. An open browser polls Goal Hint for those projections. A visit makes no API-Football request. [Worker entry point](../next-js/src/workers/live.ts), [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Matches reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-feed.ts), [Match Details reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-detail.ts).

## Fixture discovery and results

The following are normal worker targets for the coordinated mode used in the recorded production rollout. Dates are Kampala reporting dates. Quota, provider failures and incomplete responses can delay individual updates. [Worker schedule](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runner.ts), [Result policy](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-policy.ts), [Hosted rollout](../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-rollout-2026-11-20.md#matches-and-forecast-release).

| Work | Normal cadence | Fetch and update mechanism |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily schedule | 00:00 Kampala time (21:00 UTC), with catch-up for the latest eligible slot on startup | One durable selection job imports `/fixtures?date=YYYY-MM-DD&timezone=Africa/Kampala` across eight dates on standard coverage, including the extra day for the seven-day visitor window. The Free plan attempts two dates. The catalog stores verified in-scope fixtures even when forecast capacity is exhausted. [Selection trigger](../next-js/src/server/selection/selection-trigger.ts), [Selection service](../next-js/src/server/selection/selection-service.ts), [Plan limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts). |
| Hourly schedule reconciliation | 01:00–23:00 Kampala time when the account has at least 7,500 daily requests and positive forecast capacity | Rechecks today and eligible forward dates. The coordinated path can reuse complete future-date receipts for up to three hours. Known fixtures omitted from a complete date response can be checked by `/fixtures?id=` or `/fixtures?ids=`, up to two batches of 20. An additional rotation checks up to two competition-season scopes per hour for upcoming, undated and postponed fixtures, reusing verified complete scopes for 24 hours. [Runtime eligibility](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Receipt reuse](../next-js/src/server/selection/selection-mysql-store.ts), [Reconciliation](../next-js/src/server/selection/selection-fixture-reconciliation.ts). |
| Live score, status and match clock | Every 60 seconds while active, every five minutes while idle | One shared `/fixtures?live=all&timezone=Africa/Kampala` request covers the configured competition scope. The active window starts 15 minutes before an expected kickoff and covers tracked live fixtures up to three hours after kickoff. A verified complete response can discover new in-scope fixtures. Disappearance starts a verification path. [Result service](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-service.ts), [Live discovery import](../next-js/src/server/live/live-discovery-import.ts), [Result policy](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-policy.ts). |
| Current date and dedicated result checks | Current-date sweep hourly. Active ID checks normally every 60 seconds when due. | The result poller also reads `/fixtures?date=` and groups due missing or contradictory observations into requests of up to 20 IDs. Overdue unresolved results are normally checked hourly for 48 hours from kickoff or first tracking, then daily with an investigation state. Confirmed finals are normally eligible for correction every 10 minutes in the first six hours, then every six hours through day three. [Result policy](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-policy.ts), [Plan limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Result store](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-mysql-store.ts). |
| Historical schedule and match facts | Eligible archive job slots every 30 minutes | Bounded low-priority jobs resume date, season, round and fixture-detail progress. Date repair works backward from yesterday. Partial coverage, unavailable data and deferred work are recorded separately. This is a queue cadence, with completion governed by the shared quota and provider coverage. [History jobs](../next-js/src/server/football/history-import-jobs.ts), [History plan](../next-js/dev-plan/309-verified-football-history.md), [Result sync runbook](../next-js/docs/fixture-result-sync.md#cadence-and-quotas). |

Collection passes through the shared provider-day quota and pacing limiter. The result poller uses a database lease and records attempt and retry times. Capacity pressure can stretch active checks to two or five minutes and slow enrichment. Outside coordinated mode, the account-sized baseline can poll the broad live feed every 15 seconds at sufficient capacity and disable it on smaller plans, where date checks supply live status. This baseline can also reuse a complete date sweep for a live slot when all expected fixtures were freshly confirmed. The owned local profile uses a five-minute minimum date interval. [Capacity policy](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Result service](../next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-service.ts), [Result sync runbook](../next-js/docs/fixture-result-sync.md).

`FootballFixture` holds the canonical kickoff, teams, competition and status. Import receipts and result observations retain provenance. Accepted changes update the stored result and can requeue settlement. Regulation, extra-time and penalty shootout scores are verified and stored separately where confirmed. Missing or contradictory evidence can leave a fixture pending with its provider status and next check preserved. [Database schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Result write path](../next-js/src/server/results/result-write.ts), [Public status freshness](../next-js/src/server/results/result-status-freshness.ts), [Settlement and visibility report](../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-publication-settlement-2026-11-20.md).

## Match observations and details

`GOAL_HINT_LIVE_DETAILS_ENABLED` defaults off. The separate detail poller requires an eligible paid plan with at least 7,500 usable requests per day, at least 60 requests per minute and the required authority. When eligible, it budgets up to 20% of the usable provider day for detail collection and covers fixtures with or without a prediction. [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Plan limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Detail quota](../next-js/src/server/live/live-detail-quota.ts).

| Observation | Coordinated cadence and source |
| --- | --- |
| Live events and team statistics | Target every two minutes per eligible live fixture, with slower collection under quota pressure. The collector reuses compatible recent observations from the broad live sweep, then requests `/fixtures?ids=` in batches of up to 20 for remaining fixtures. Explicit coverage and missing sections can trigger `/fixtures/events`, `/fixtures/statistics` and, where supported, first- and second-half statistics via `half=true`. |
| Lineups and player data | Covered upcoming fixtures become eligible for lineup checks 90 minutes before kickoff and can be retried every 15 minutes until confirmed. The collector reuses embedded lineups or player statistics where available. Verified coverage and a missing section can trigger `/fixtures/lineups` or `/fixtures/players`. Player statistics follow the live or final detail checks. |
| Final match details | The first check becomes due 15 minutes after the first observed final, and a later check is due at 24 hours. Missing eligible final details can be retried within the three-day collection window. |

The noncoordinated collector normally uses separate single-fixture statistics and events requests each minute while live and checks recent finals every 15 minutes. The owned local profile can batch details. [Detail timing policy](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-contract.ts), [Collector](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-service.ts), [Due fixture selection](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-mysql-store.ts), [Match detail runbook](../next-js/docs/live-match-details.md#central-collection-and-request-budget).

API-Football describes upstream updates of about 15 seconds for events and one minute for team statistics where coverage is available. Those updates reach stored Match Details through the worker checks above. [API-Football endpoint guide](https://www.api-football.com/news/post/how-to-get-started-with-api-football-the-complete-beginners-guide).

`LiveMatchSnapshot` keeps verified events and team statistics with section-specific retrieval and provider clocks. The stored occurrence is tied to the fixture, kickoff, teams and applicable prediction cycle. `FixtureFacts` retains eligible lineup, player and richer statistics data for downstream use. During live play, a section with a retrieval clock older than five minutes can appear stale while the previous values remain visible. An absent section can show waiting, unavailable or partial coverage. The Lineups and Players tabs load stored Match Insights evidence on demand under their own access rules. The polled live snapshot supplies the separate Events and Statistics panels. [Snapshot reader](../next-js/src/server/live-match/live-match-read.ts), [Match facts store](../next-js/src/server/football/fixture-facts-store.ts), [Insights reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/insights-read.ts), [Details tabs](../next-js/src/components/match/details/touchline-tabs.tsx).

An optional coordinated context job runs hourly after verified discovery of the current day. It reuses fresh data and rotates bounded requests for standings, injuries, team statistics and other reference data. Its normal freshness targets include one hour for standings, four hours for injuries, 12 hours for team statistics and one day for active player-statistics data. [Context schedule and source windows](../next-js/src/server/football/current-day-refresh.ts).

## What the browser refreshes

- **Matches** opens on Today. The standalone `GET /api/matches` defaults to a 30-day range. The ordinary list applies its published-prediction eligibility rules, including eligible locked cards, and the separate offline fixture projection can include fixtures without forecasts. **Match Details** and `GET /api/matches/{id}` read the stored fixture, applicable forecast and eligible match observations. `GET /api/matches/{id}/insights?section=…` supplies stored insight sections as needed. Live events and statistics are read outside the immutable forecast cache. [Feed query](../next-js/src/domain/feed-query.ts), [Matches reader](../next-js/src/server/matches/public-feed.ts), [Detail service](../next-js/src/server/matches/detail-service.ts), [Insights API](../next-js/src/server/matches/insights-service.ts).
- **Open views** refresh every 20 seconds while live, while the applicable run is updating, or when a scheduled kickoff is within 30 minutes either side of now. Otherwise they refresh every 60 seconds. Hidden or offline views pause and resume on visibility or reconnection. Historical revision inspection retains its selected occurrence. When running timing is available, the displayed clock advances locally from the last accepted observation. [Refresh policy](../next-js/src/domain/live-refresh.ts), [Client refresh runbook](../next-js/docs/live-client-refresh.md), [Match clock](../next-js/src/domain/match-clock.ts).

## Production observations, 26–27 November 2026

The sample covers 08:00 Kampala time on 26 November through 08:00 on 27 November. It uses read-only production selection runs, import receipts, result batches, detail snapshots and quota dispatches, plus worker logs and follow-up checks of the signed-out site. The worker heartbeat at 07:59 confirmed coordinated mode and both the result and live-detail pollers attached. Production cron checks for a worker every five minutes. The running worker owns the collection intervals. [Database schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [VPS worker runbook](../next-js/docs/deployment/how-to-deploy.md#vps-live-football-worker), [Production Matches](https://goalhint.com/en/matches).

| Work | Observed in production |
| --- | --- |
| Schedule and fixture discovery | All 24 selection slots committed, covering midnight and 23 hourly runs. Five committed with partial coverage across their date range. Hourly runs committed a median of 3 minutes 52 seconds after their slot. The 27 November midnight run committed at 00:13:29 with all eight date imports complete. The current-day import was complete in 22 of 24 slots, with those receipts arriving a median of 21 seconds after the scheduled slot. The 03:00 and 04:00 slots on 27 November lacked a complete current-day import. |
| Live scores, status and clock | The result ledger retained 697 broad live batches, including 678 successful and 19 failed batches. Successful retained batches were 90.6 seconds apart at the median and 112.6 seconds at the 90th percentile. Three fixtures with 69–81 live observations each had per-fixture median gaps of 83.7–85.7 seconds. These observations can confirm an unchanged score or status. The ledger omits successful empty polls, so it cannot establish the observed idle cadence. |
| Dated and fixture-ID result checks | The separate date result poller recorded 23 successful batches and one minute-limit failure. Consecutive successful date batches were 61 minutes 17 seconds apart at the median. Of 749 fixture-ID batches, 717 succeeded. |
| Match details and supplements | The live-detail poller attached with its two-minute live target. The quota ledger recorded 11,357 detail dispatches and 11,295 successes. It recorded batched `/fixtures`, lineup, player and statistics requests, with zero separate detail-priority `/fixtures/events` requests. A single fixture response can carry embedded events and a batch can serve several matches. |
| Signed-out Matches and browser refresh | The one-day API returned HTTP 200 with 335 known fixtures and 323 matching rows. Its latest authoritative date receipt at 08:00 was from 07:00:25. The next run recorded failed date coverage at 08:01 with `missing-source-provenance`. At 08:02, the API still served the same counts with non-authoritative coverage. In a visible Chromium tab with no live fixtures, two background requests started 64.5 seconds apart. The second started 60.0 seconds after the first finished. |

Across 918 latest final or abandoned snapshots whose first-final and collection timestamps both fell within the window, the median delay from first recorded final to the retained snapshot was 15 minutes 48 seconds. The 90th percentile was about 21 minutes 45 seconds. Both statistics and events had `available` status in 60 of those snapshots, all collected within 16 minutes of the first final. In 841 snapshots, events were available and statistics were unavailable. In a signed-out spot check of three fixtures that kicked off at 23:00 on 26 November, detail sections had been retrieved at 01:04:34–01:10:16 the next day. The result sync times for the same fixtures had since advanced to 06:43:56–06:52:51.
