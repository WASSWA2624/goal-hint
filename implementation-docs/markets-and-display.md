# Markets and display

Goal Hint uses the same market identities on desktop and mobile. A **family** groups related predictions. A **market** fixes the period, subject, line and grading rule. A **selection** identifies one outcome within it. For example, full-time home team Over 2.5 and full-time match Over 2.5 are separate selections. The registry defines 30 families. Each screen reads an eligible subset of stored forecasts or bookmaker offers. [Market registry](../next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [Prediction engine](prediction-engine.md).

## Base markets

The four base forecast groups use regulation time, including stoppage time. Extra time and penalty shootouts are outside that period. A fixture can have some groups published while others are unavailable.

| Family | Compact display | Selection meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Match result (1X2) | `1`, `X`, `2` | Home win, draw, away win. |
| Double chance | `1X`, `X2`, `12` | Home or draw, draw or away, home or away. These overlap, so the three probabilities sum to two. |
| Both teams to score (BTTS) | `Yes`, `No` | Each team scores at least once, or at least one team scores zero. |
| Total goals (2.5) | `O 2.5`, `U 2.5` | Three or more match goals, or zero to two. The extended binary 2.5 identity is the same event, and Match Details suppresses the duplicate row. |

Verified API-Football forecast fallback can supply full-time 1X2 and double chance derived from it. A bookmaker catalogue or price can establish an exact offer independently of forecast availability. [Base groups](../next-js/src/domain/markets.ts), [Provider scope](../next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [Display codes](../next-js/docs/prediction-outcomes.md).

## Registered markets and conditional availability

Active Local Engine profiles cover full time. A first- or second-half forecast requires verified period evidence and a separately qualified model for the precise market. The qualified score-grid evaluator can produce exact estimates conditionally. Publication of the legacy AI score distribution extension is separately authorized and uses the reviewed count definitions in that revision. Catalogue membership, a registered rule, or a completed score alone cannot supply a missing forecast. [Forecast evaluator](../next-js/src/server/predictions/forecast-market-evaluator.ts), [Publication authority](../next-js/src/server/live/live-authorities.ts), [Count and score definitions](../next-js/src/domain/goal-market-definitions.ts).

| Registered scope | Selections and current access |
| --- | --- |
| Regulation team to score, clean sheet | Each team has Yes/No. Team to score asks whether the named team scores at least once. Clean sheet asks whether it concedes zero. The Local Engine-first evaluator marks these as unsupported publication projections. |
| First- and second-half goal markets | Registered equivalents include 1X2, double chance, draw no bet, Asian and European handicaps, BTTS, correct score, team to score, clean sheet, win to nil, match/team binary and Asian totals, exact count, ranges and odd/even. Availability follows the qualified source and stored publication for that exact period. |
| Half-time / full-time, highest-scoring half, scoring in both halves | Half-time/full-time has all nine pairs of Home/Draw/Away, e.g. `X / 2`. Highest-scoring half has First half, Equal and Second half. Scoring in both halves has Yes/No for Match, Home or Away. All require verified scores for the relevant halves and a qualified period model. |

Counts need matching period, metric and counting rules. Independent statistic forecasts are unavailable in the current model. |
| First or last team event | Home, Away or None for the specified first/last event, with a complete verified event sequence. The independent event-order model is unavailable. |
| Player scorer and player event | Yes/No for the named player and anytime, first or last event. Player scorer uses goals. Player event supports the other registered event metrics. Event metrics are corners, yellow cards, red cards, cards, penalties, goals and assists. An authorized **Market-implied** extension can retain a separately labeled estimate derived by removing the margin from a complete, verified selection group from one bookmaker. Asian lines carry a **conditional payout price** basis. Ordinary groups carry a **normalized bookmaker price** basis. These records are distinct from model-event probabilities. The ongoing Local Engine-first binding does not supply an implied extension. If one exists in a stored snapshot, the Matches exact-outcomes picker, Match Details forecast rows and both slip surfaces exclude it. - The prediction label represents the exact saved selection. Shared outcome helpers supply abbreviations, descriptions and accessible names.
