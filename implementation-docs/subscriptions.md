# Subscriptions and content access

## Purpose and scope

Subscriptions grant time-limited access to the signed-in account. Active packages share the same access tier, and eligible devices read the account's current entitlement. **Account → Subscription** shows the current package and period. **Subscribe** handles purchase and renewal entry from Account or a locked feature. The Flutter shell uses the same web screens and account entitlement. [Account](../next-js/src/app/_components/account-route.tsx), [Subscribe](../next-js/src/app/_components/subscribe-route.tsx), [Flutter web shell](../flutter/lib/web/web_app_shell.dart), [authentication](authentication.md), [payments](payments.md).

## Current implementation

The standard package migration seeds these offers when their durations are absent. Current prices and availability come from the stored `SubscriptionPlan` catalogue. [Package seed](../next-js/prisma/migrations/20261021103000_standard_subscription_packages/migration.sql), [plan reader and editor](../next-js/src/server/members/plans.ts).

| Initial label | Duration | Initial price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | 24 hours | UGX 1,000 |
| Weekly | 7 days | UGX 5,000 |
| Monthly | 30 days | UGX 20,000 |

The owner uses **Admin → Plans** to edit the names, durations, prices and availability of offers. Deletion is available for a plan without payment, period or auto-renewal references. Affected requests and issued periods retain their original plan snapshots. [Plans screen](../next-js/src/app/(owner)/admin/plans/page.tsx), [plan persistence](../next-js/src/server/members/plans.ts), [purchase snapshot](../next-js/src/server/members/checkout.ts).

### Effective access

| Viewer or policy | Effective server access |
| --- | --- |
| Guest, blocked account or invalid session, either mode | Public fixtures, completed predictions and Best Slips previews. Protected actions require login and eligible access. |
| Paywall **Off**, confirmed settings and eligible signed-in member | Full Matches, Best Slips and Builder access. New purchases, trials and automatic renewals are paused. |
| Paywall **On**, eligible signed-in member with no active period | Public projection and the existing subscription prompt. |
| Paywall **On**, active paid, trial, referral or admin-granted period | Full content with an eligible session. Access starts at the recorded start instant and expires exactly at the end instant. Future and revoked periods provide no current entitlement. |
| Active administrator, either mode | Full content through the administrator session. Admin accounts do not purchase or claim member trials. |

The public Matches projection keeps fixtures and completed predictions. Unfinished forecasts, probabilities, odds and detailed prediction history are gated. The server restricts unfinished outcome filters and probability sorting that would expose those fields. Match Insights checks completion before granting public access. Builder generation requires full access. Best Slips can show fixture previews, individually completed selections and complete slips under the corresponding access rules. The server reads current policy for member entitlement and can return `503` when it cannot verify access for a protected request. [Viewer access](../next-js/src/server/members/viewer-access.ts), [gated reads](../next-js/src/server/members/gated-reads.ts), [match redaction](../next-js/src/domain/paywall.ts), [slip previews](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-preview.ts), [Builder](slip-builder.md), [automatic slips](automatic-slips.md).

## End-to-end flows

1. **Start a trial.** Eligible phone-and-password registration issues a trial in the account-creation transaction when Paid Access is On and the configured duration is positive. The trial uses the account's creation-time window. Legacy verified accounts with an unused offer can activate it through the trial control while that original window is open. First paid access forfeits an unused legacy offer or queues after an active trial. Previously issued dates stay recorded as members sign in again or change devices. [Registration](../next-js/src/server/members/member-auth.ts), [trial and period creation](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [trial endpoint](../next-js/src/app/api/account/trial/route.ts).
2. **Choose and pay.** Unlock actions for a Match, Builder or a slip carry a validated feature path into login or checkout. Eligible signed-in members with current access continue to the feature. Others can choose an active package with usable funding. Checkout grants access on verified manual approval, verified Flutterwave success or a completed wallet purchase. Top-ups add credit for a later purchase, and earned days use a separate redemption. Fresh registrations go to Matches. [Subscribe route](../next-js/src/app/_components/subscribe-route.tsx), [member navigation](../next-js/src/domain/member-navigation.ts), [authentication return routing](../next-js/src/server/members/member-auth-http.ts), [payment and evidence flow](payments.md), [wallet and referrals](wallet-and-referrals.md).
3. **Use and expire.** `AccessCode` stores the entitlement with its account, source, dates, recorded plan and revocation history. Paid time uses exact 24-hour units and normally appends to the current unexpired chain. Account Overview and Subscription show the plan, state and expiry. Subscription also lists queued periods with their dates. Available actions include **View payment**, **Subscribe**, **Extend** and **Renew**. The private session summary can include contiguous unrevoked time. The read-time access check uses the current instant and removes a period from entitlement at its exact end. [Account panels](../next-js/src/components/members/account-panels.tsx), [queued packages](../next-js/src/app/_components/account-route.tsx), [period calculation](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [viewer entitlement](../next-js/src/server/members/viewer-access.ts).
4. **Renew or turn renewal off.** **Account → Wallet** lets an eligible member opt into wallet auto-renewal with an active plan, accepted terms and positive spendable credit. A due renewal can buy the selected plan at its current price if spendable credit covers it. The one-hour renewal lead is measured from the chain end. The account lock and wallet request key prevent a second charge for the same chain end. Maintenance covers recent lapses up to one day old. A later eligible member visit can trigger renewal after a longer lapse, starting the period at that time. Trial-only chains and chains ending in a still-active legacy referral grant skip renewal purchases. Turning auto-renewal off remains available during sales pauses and preserves issued time. [Wallet controls](../next-js/src/components/members/account-wallet.tsx), [renewal transaction](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [maintenance sweep](../next-js/src/server/members/auto-renew.ts), [wallet funding](wallet-and-referrals.md).
5. **Administer a period.** **Admin → Codes** supports manual grants, eligible extensions of 1–60 days and reasoned revocations. Eligible legacy payment reversals follow their payment workflow. Period operations retain elapsed history and may move affected queued time. Account-level actions in **Users** include blocks and administrative renewal disablement. [Codes screen](../next-js/src/app/(owner)/admin/codes/page.tsx), [period operations](../next-js/src/server/admin/codes-http.ts), [period rechaining](../next-js/src/domain/subscription-period.ts), [user actions](../next-js/src/server/admin/users.ts).

## User administration

- **Admin → Users** at `/admin/users` lists registered accounts regardless of payment status. Search matches phone, account ID, package, access code, payment reference and private note. Advanced filters cover account status, current access, package and logins within the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. SQL applies the filters before counting and paging, returns at most 100 accounts, and orders by most recent sign-in with account ID as the tie-breaker. Rows show the account identity, active, queued or no access, and last login in Kampala time. Same-day logins display the time, older logins include the date, and missing logins have an explicit label. [Filter controls](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-directory-parts.tsx), [directory queries](../next-js/src/server/admin/user-directory.ts).
- Select the account identity to open its detail or use a direct account link. **Overview** shows credit balance and account ID, with access, payments, credit history and device information in their own tabs. The footer keeps individual grant, number change, block or unblock, device logout and eligible password-reset actions. **More actions** opens the private admin note. **Delete or erase** opens the confirmation flow with the existing financial and audit retention rules. [User detail](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-detail.tsx), [authentication](authentication.md).
- **Show More** appends up to 100 accounts after the last loaded sign-in timestamp and account ID, using the current filters and deduplicating by account ID. **Refresh** runs the authenticated query for the selected starting page. On success it updates the count and replaces the appended results while preserving the selected account and its active tab. The visible rows and selection stay in place during refresh and transient failure. Initial Users reads have a shared eight-second budget after authentication. The workspace shows completed results and offers an exact retry for each unavailable region. The directory, account detail and plans reads have 15-second browser deadlines. An explicit account link can still open if the directory load fails. An unexpected Users render error offers a retry within the owner workspace. An unavailable session verification returns `503` without serving private data. A confirmed missing or invalid administrator session returns `401` and clears the workspace. [User workspace](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-workspace.tsx), [administrator guard](../next-js/src/server/admin/admin-http.ts), [initial reads](../next-js/src/server/admin/user-workspace.ts), [render recovery](../next-js/src/app/(owner)/admin/users/error.tsx).
- **Select all** checks every loaded user, including those already appended by **Show More**. Accounts loaded afterward require another selection. Changing the filter or starting page clears the selection. **Manage** offers free access grants, blocks, unblocks, device sign-out, erasure and permanent deletion for eligible selected users. Phone number and password changes use individual actions. [User directory controls](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-directory.tsx), [bulk controls](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-bulk-actions.tsx).
- `/api/admin/users` handles the authenticated directory query, `/api/admin/users/[id]` handles individual accounts, and `/api/admin/users/bulk` accepts up to 100 accounts per request. A large confirmed selection is sent sequentially in batches. The server rechecks authorization for each target and makes account changes independently. The interface reports individual outcomes together and refreshes the affected reads once. A grant retried with the same request ID preserves the period already issued for that account. [Bulk request handling](../next-js/src/server/admin/users-http.ts), [bulk controls](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-bulk-actions.tsx).
- Permanent deletion removes eligible accounts and their linked credentials, sessions, verifications, personal slips, eligible payments and evidence, and access periods in one transaction. The deletion audit records the operation without a deleted account identifier. Open payments, receipt accounting, wallet history, cash grants and number-move dependencies can require retention. Erasure removes personal data, revokes sessions, and preserves the accounting and access history. [Deletion and retention](../next-js/src/server/admin/delete-user.ts), [payments](payments.md).
- Bookmarked `/admin/subscribers` pages and calls under `/api/admin/subscribers` permanently redirect to their Users equivalents. The 28 November rollout verified the redirect paths, bookmarked queries and guest access guards on staging and production. Signed-in account actions and retention rules have separate staging and database coverage. [Route configuration](../next-js/next.config.ts), [Users rollout](deployment-and-version-control.md#users-route-compatibility), [account administration verification](deployment-and-version-control.md#user-administration-production-verification).

## Rules and edge cases

- A missing `SubscriptionSettings` row defaults to Paid Access Off with a two-day trial configured for On mode. **Admin → Settings → Access** controls the switch and trial duration. A duration of zero disables new grants. Changing the switch preserves issued periods. Checkout readiness is evaluated separately from content access, and existing requests have their own completion path while new sales pause. [Settings defaults](../next-js/src/domain/subscription-settings.ts), [settings interface](../next-js/src/components/admin/settings-editor.tsx), [settings service](../next-js/src/server/members/settings.ts), [checkout readiness](../next-js/src/server/members/sales-readiness.ts).
- New auto-renewal enrollment checks checkout readiness. Execution of an existing enrollment checks Paid Access On, the active selected plan, due-period eligibility and spendable wallet credit. A failure in other checkout-readiness prerequisites can leave existing wallet renewal available. [Renewal enrollment and execution](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [wallet and renewal rules](wallet-and-referrals.md).
- The content resolver retains a compatibility check for a valid legacy analytics session. The Admin and Analytics dashboards use the current member administrator session. [Viewer access](../next-js/src/server/members/viewer-access.ts), [dashboard authorization](../next-js/src/server/analytics/analytics-http.ts).
- Account and Subscribe can refresh their display as requests resolve or a period expires. The server checks entitlement at protected content entry points. [Approval and expiry refresh](../next-js/src/components/members/payment-status.tsx), [access resolver](../next-js/src/server/members/viewer-access.ts).

## Gaps and limits

The deployed catalogue, prices, Paid Access switch, trial duration and provider readiness depend on live settings. The initial migration and defaults describe starting values. Documented staging and production access checks apply to the settings observed during those releases. Members can disable future wallet renewals. An owner handles period revocation and eligible legacy reversals. [Release verification](deployment-and-version-control.md), [period controls](../next-js/src/server/admin/codes-http.ts), [payment reconciliation](payments.md).
