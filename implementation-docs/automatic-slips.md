# Automatic slips

## Purpose and scope

**Slips** is the navigation label for the **Best Slips** screen at `/en/best-slips`. It reads published recommendations prepared by a private worker. **Builder** at `/en/slips` generates a separate set on request. [Navigation](../next-js/src/components/navigation/navigation-destinations.ts), [Best Slips route](../next-js/src/app/_components/best-slips-route.tsx), [Slip Builder](slip-builder.md).

## Generation and eligibility

1. The stored publisher reads existing database forecasts, fixtures and odds. `npm run worker:slips -- --stored --once` processes the current Kampala half-hour slot. Without `--once`, the process waits for successive `:00` and `:30` boundaries. The binding needs an approved database, a configured competition scope and verified public odds rights. `GOAL_HINT_COMPETITION_IDS` supplies scope and `GOAL_HINT_FOOTBALL_ODDS_RIGHTS_REF` identifies the rights approval. [Worker](../next-js/src/workers/slips.ts), [Publisher command](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-publication-command.ts), [Runtime policy](../next-js/src/server/config/runtime-policy.ts).
2. A dated set begins processing at midnight in `Africa/Kampala` and owns fixtures kicking off from 03:00 that day through 03:00 the next day, excluding the end boundary. Early morning checks consider the previous and current sets in that order. A new leg needs at least 30 minutes before kickoff, including at commit. By the 02:30 slot, the previous set has no remaining eligible kickoff. Fixture status must be scheduled with a matching open prediction cycle and no blocking lifecycle issue or trusted early-start evidence. [Clock and operational window](../next-js/src/domain/best-slip-publication-hour.ts), [Publisher](../next-js/src/server/slips/daily-slip-publication.ts), [Cohort](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-cohort.ts), [Forecast binding](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-forecast.ts).
3. For each eligible fixture, the worker considers its current priced featured outcome from a numerically qualified Local Engine revision. The featured policy applies a 60% minimum. Retained offers must match the fixture, kickoff, market and selection, including the exact line and side when applicable. They must have current, usable price evidence, with a 12-hour freshness window for pre-match reference odds. Verified alternative bookmakers can price the same outcome. Each slip uses one bookmaker. Eligible outcomes cover the base result, double chance, BTTS and total-goals families plus supported full-time match and team half-goal totals. [Featured decision](../next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [Candidate and price checks](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slip-recommendations.ts), [Reference odds](../next-js/src/domain/reference-odds.ts), [Slip markets](../next-js/src/domain/slip-markets.ts), [Prediction engine](prediction-engine.md).

## Combination search and publication

- The six exact odds bands are **2.50–5.00**, **5.00–10.00**, **10.00–20.00**, **20.00–50.00**, **50.00–100.00** and **100.00–200.00**. Each lower edge is inclusive. Upper edges are exclusive except 200.00. The engine multiplies exact decimal quotes before rounding for display, so a rounded boundary label can differ from the stored band's exact membership. A slip has one to eight matches. [Odds bands](../next-js/src/domain/best-slip-odds-bands.ts), [Selection logic](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips.ts).
- The engine considers bands in their defined order and groups fixtures by observed kickoff spacing. It prefers combinations that can form from earlier groups and stay within nearby groups. The earliest leg must be within the default six-hour release window. Other legs may extend beyond that window. Among comparable groups it ranks by a conservative joint lower bound, `max(0, sum of leg probabilities − number of legs + 1)`, then stronger constituents, more conservative individual odds, and other exact-price, match-count and timing tie-breaks. The bound avoids an independence assumption and is an internal ranking input. [Selector](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips.ts), [Shared search](../next-js/src/domain/slip-builder.ts).
- Each half-hour slot can add one nonempty batch of up to 32 slips per band and set. Later slots can append more. Modern sets have no ten-slip daily cap. The worker pages a bounded kickoff-ordered cohort and reports search or coverage limits. Empty runs add nothing. The store rejects repeated fixtures across bands in the same set and checks adjacent sets for previous allocations, including when a fixture is rescheduled. [Publisher](../next-js/src/server/slips/daily-slip-publication.ts), [Publication store](../next-js/src/server/slips/published-slips.ts).
- A repeatable-read transaction locks the date range, fixture and odds state, rechecks original forecast and quote eligibility, and appends the batch, slip snapshots and leg identities atomically. Slot and combination identities protect against retries. Published selections, prices, ranks and timestamps are sealed and retained. The tables are `DailySlipPublication`, `DailyBestSlipAppendBatch`, `PublishedSlip`, `PublishedSlipLeg` and `DailyBestSlipCombination`. Older formats keep their original limits and public rank links. [Prisma schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Operational-window migration](../next-js/prisma/migrations/20261126120000_operational_best_slip_windows/migration.sql), [MariaDB overlay](../next-js/prisma/mariadb/20261126120000_operational_best_slip_windows.sql).

## Browsing, access and outcomes

`GET /api/best-slips` and the page read the sealed archive. They default to Today, all results and the 2.50–5.00 band. Date or date-range, odds-band, average-probability, match-count and outcome filters affect browsing. Period statistics apply before the outcome filter and pagination. Before 03:00, Today can include previous-set slips whose legs are still pre-match and within its remaining window. Direct links use `/en/best-slips/{day}/{rank}`. [Query rules](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips-query.ts), [Browse reader](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slips-browse.ts), [API handler](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slips-http.ts).

The six outcome cards sit directly under the header. The date tiles, selected-day calendar trigger and filter button share the next row. The desktop fixture status badge shows the active match clock, including added time and paused phases, when an observation supplies it. [Browse filters](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips-filters.tsx), [Desktop fixture row](../next-js/src/components/slips/desktop-slip/desktop-slip-row.tsx).

Public summaries include total odds, arithmetic average leg probability and any originally stored joint estimate. Guests receive safe fixture previews and leg picks with verified completion in both paid-access modes. Full access opens unfinished selections for eligible signed-in viewers under the shared policy. The reader checks access before releasing the projection. Live, elapsed kickoff, schedule changes and trusted early-play evidence can make a slip unavailable for placement while preserving its archive record. Outcome reads grade original cycle and revision identities. Cancelled or postponed occurrences can become void according to recorded cycle evidence. The original total remains the archived price. [Access projection](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-preview.ts), [Forecast and results](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-forecast.ts), [Slip status](../next-js/src/domain/slip-status.ts), [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md).

Share resolves the requested publication through the same audited snapshot reader. The selected-slip lookup converts MariaDB's widened unsigned rank before calculating the snapshot offset. This restores share preparation for the published archive while keeping the existing access checks. The drawer prepares images for download and provides manual sharing instructions on browsers without native share. [Publication reader](../next-js/src/server/slips/published-slips.ts), [Share handler](../next-js/src/server/slips/shared-slips-http.ts), [Slip sharing](../next-js/docs/slip-sharing.md).

## Verification and limits

Focused tests cover the operational window, chronological search, odds boundaries, concurrent allocation, append-only history and access. Current worker code stores no qualified whole-slip estimate for new batches. It has no bookmaker payout feed to calculate effective settled odds after a void. Coverage, bookmaker availability and the running hosted schedule depend on the target environment.
