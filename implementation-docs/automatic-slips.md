# Automatic slips

## Purpose and scope

**Slips** is the navigation label for the **Best Slips** screen at `/en/best-slips`. It reads published recommendations prepared by a private worker. **Builder** at `/en/slips` generates a separate set on request. [Navigation](../next-js/src/components/navigation/navigation-destinations.ts), [Best Slips route](../next-js/src/app/_components/best-slips-route.tsx), [Slip Builder](slip-builder.md).

## Generation and eligibility

1. The stored publisher reads existing database forecasts, fixtures and odds. `npm run worker:slips -- --stored --once` processes the current Kampala half-hour slot. Without `--once`, the process waits for successive `:00` and `:30` boundaries. The binding needs an approved database, a configured competition scope and verified public odds rights. `GOAL_HINT_COMPETITION_IDS` supplies scope and `GOAL_HINT_FOOTBALL_ODDS_RIGHTS_REF` identifies the rights approval. [Worker](../next-js/src/workers/slips.ts), [Publisher command](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-publication-command.ts), [Runtime policy](../next-js/src/server/config/runtime-policy.ts).

   With `GOAL_HINT_SLIP_ODDS_REFRESH_ENABLED=true` on both workers, the football worker schedules a targeted pre-match refresh at `:25` and `:55` Kampala time for the next publication slot. It checks up to 12 upcoming fixtures with a qualified forecast and missing or stale exact odds under the existing quota and rights checks. The publisher waits for that slot's successful durable refresh result for up to five minutes past its scheduled start. A verified empty candidate list succeeds without provider traffic. A failed or overdue refresh causes the publisher to skip that slot. The three-hour date sweep has its own schedule. [Refresh job](../next-js/src/server/odds/slip-odds-refresh-job.ts), [Slot and wait](../next-js/src/server/odds/slip-odds-refresh-slot.ts).

2. A dated set begins processing at midnight in `Africa/Kampala` and owns fixtures kicking off from 03:00 that day through 03:00 the next day, excluding the end boundary. Early morning checks consider the previous and current sets in that order. A new leg needs at least 30 minutes before kickoff, including at commit. By the 02:30 slot, the previous set has no remaining eligible kickoff. Fixture status must be scheduled with a matching open prediction cycle and no blocking lifecycle issue or trusted early-start evidence. [Clock and operational window](../next-js/src/domain/best-slip-publication-hour.ts), [Publisher](../next-js/src/server/slips/daily-slip-publication.ts), [Cohort](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-cohort.ts), [Forecast binding](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-forecast.ts).
3. For each eligible fixture, the worker considers its current priced featured outcome from a numerically qualified Local Engine revision. The featured policy applies a 60% minimum. Retained offers must match the fixture, kickoff, market and selection, including the exact line and side when applicable. They must have current, usable price evidence, with a 12-hour freshness window for pre-match reference odds. Verified alternative bookmakers can price the same outcome. Each slip uses one bookmaker. Eligible outcomes cover the base result, double chance, BTTS and total-goals families plus supported full-time match and team half-goal totals. [Featured decision](../next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [Candidate and price checks](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slip-recommendations.ts), [Reference odds](../next-js/src/domain/reference-odds.ts), [Slip markets](../next-js/src/domain/slip-markets.ts), [Prediction engine](prediction-engine.md).

## Combination search and publication

- The six exact odds bands are **2.50–5.00**, **5.00–10.00**, **10.00–20.00**, **20.00–50.00**, **50.00–100.00** and **100.00–200.00**. Each lower edge is inclusive. Upper edges are exclusive except 200.00. The engine multiplies exact decimal quotes before rounding for display, so a rounded boundary label can differ from the stored band's exact membership. A slip has one to eight matches. [Odds bands](../next-js/src/domain/best-slip-odds-bands.ts), [Selection logic](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips.ts).
- The earliest leg must fall within the default six-hour release window. Later legs can extend through the operational set. The engine searches elapsed kickoff spans in 15-minute steps, starting with 0–15 minutes, then over 15 through 30, then over 30 through 45, and so on. It searches each bookmaker's quotes within each step using one search budget per odds band. In each step it ranks by the conservative joint lower bound `max(0, sum of leg probabilities − number of legs + 1)`, then the exact shortest span at the same bound. Constituent risk, distinct verified bookmaker support and price distinguish remaining ties within an odds band. The bound is an internal ranking input and avoids an independence assumption. [Selector](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips.ts), [Shared search](../next-js/src/domain/slip-builder.ts).
- Each open odds band searches the same retained pool before allocation. The publisher assigns fixtures to the narrowest eligible span step first, with the highest bound winning within that step. Unused fixtures remain available for wider steps. Exact ties across odds bands rotate by slot. Each half-hour slot can add one nonempty batch of up to 32 slips per band and set. Later slots can append more. The publisher reports retained feasible, selected and committed counts, fixture conflicts, empty-band reasons and bounded search counts in its private result. Pruned branch counts can overlap across search passes. An empty band adds no rows. [Publisher](../next-js/src/server/slips/daily-slip-publication.ts).
- When a wide candidate would carry a fixture across the release horizon, a bounded check of the same stored quotes can find an upcoming alternative in the same exact odds band. The publisher defers the wide candidate for the slot when the upcoming alternative uses an unclaimed shared fixture, fits a strictly narrower span step, and has an equal or higher conservative win bound. The gap from the wide candidate's last released kickoff to the upcoming group's first kickoff must exceed that narrower step's width. The next slot recalculates from the available fixtures and prices. [Publisher](../next-js/src/server/slips/daily-slip-publication.ts), [Upcoming comparison](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips.ts).
- A repeatable-read transaction locks the date range, fixture and odds state. It rechecks each fixture's verified kickoff and minimum lead alongside original forecast and quote eligibility before atomically appending the batch, slip snapshots and leg identities. The store checks fixture allocations across bands and adjacent sets, including fixtures rescheduled across a boundary. Slot and combination identities protect against retries. Published selections, prices, ranks and timestamps are sealed and retained. The tables are `DailySlipPublication`, `DailyBestSlipAppendBatch`, `PublishedSlip`, `PublishedSlipLeg` and `DailyBestSlipCombination`. Older formats keep their original limits and public rank links. [Publication store](../next-js/src/server/slips/published-slips.ts), [Prisma schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Operational-window migration](../next-js/prisma/migrations/20261126120000_operational_best_slip_windows/migration.sql), [MariaDB overlay](../next-js/prisma/mariadb/20261126120000_operational_best_slip_windows.sql).

## Browsing, access and outcomes

### Filters, order and layout

The page opens on Today with Pending selected in the 2.50–5.00 band. Pending groups not-started, in-progress and unresolved slips. All slips shows all outcomes in the applied cohort. Direct `GET /api/best-slips` requests without `status` default to all results. Desktop shows all six performance cards. Compact widths show Won, Pending, Lost and Others in one row. Others includes Void and Cancelled. The shared date tiles, selected-day calendar button and Filters remain below the cards. Desktop provides aligned slip legs and compact layouts stack their fields. Both use the same card summary, disclosure and original pick details. [Query rules](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips-query.ts), [API handler](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slips-http.ts), [Filters](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips-filters.tsx), [Shared slip card](../next-js/src/components/slips/slip-card.tsx), [UI implementation](ui-implementation.md).

The Filters dialog edits a draft for dates, odds band, result, displayed average leg probability and match count. **Reset Filters** changes the draft to the defaults. **Apply** validates and requests the new criteria, and closing the dialog restores focus to its trigger. Date-strip and outcome-card changes preserve the other applied criteria and return to page one. Period counts use the applied cohort before outcome filtering or pagination. Desktop shows the audited published count for a single date and odds band separately from matching rows. The compact results caption gives the applied date or range and odds band. [Filter draft](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips-filters.tsx), [Applied queries](../next-js/src/domain/best-slips-query.ts), [Summary and pagination](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips-browse-presentation.tsx).

The archive lists newer publication days first and reads modern band entries in public-rank order within each day. A legacy single-day list can use displayed average probability for its historical ranks. Before 03:00, Today can first show actionable slips from the closing set whose legs remain pre-match and within its remaining window. Show More appends up to 20 slips per request. **All dates** continues through bounded windows of up to 31 publication dates. When an audited 21st match confirms continuation, the reader returns the cursor without examining the rest of that window. All dates reports no exact historical totals. Explicit date ranges still aggregate every matching publication in the selected period. ### Refresh and feedback

`useBestSlipsBrowse` uses a same-origin, uncached GET and a 30-second deadline. It keeps the accepted filters, list and counts visible and verifies the query, day and viewer before updating. For up to six numbered pages within the response byte limit, **Show More** either receives only the next page for the exact known membership or receives one fresh window with current counts when the cohort changes. It can also end at the current last page if slips settled during the request. Longer numbered windows and the All dates cursor use the guarded page continuation and retry flow. [Browse hook](../next-js/src/components/slips/use-best-slips-browse.ts), [Page validation](../next-js/src/domain/slip-browse-window.ts), [Show More](../next-js/src/components/ui/show-more.tsx).

An accepted filter change updates the URL. **Show More** keeps the starting URL and records the loaded depth and scroll position for browser history restoration. An in-app visit commits the requested date, odds band and outcome controls beneath the shared shell. The route starts the archive read and streams its gated result to those controls. The browse API can recover an unavailable initial seed for the same query. History restoration uses the same browse operation to recover the saved depth. Direct document requests stream the controls around the server's archive and viewer reads. If the archive or viewer check fails, the inline recovery state preserves those filters and withholds unverified slips and totals. Starting a foreground retry replaces the alert with the busy state and keeps the controls visible. A valid, authorized API response for that query fills the list and counts in the same document. Foreground changes use the shared progress bar and busy regions. [Browse hook](../next-js/src/components/slips/use-best-slips-browse.ts), [Route and streamed shell](../next-js/src/app/_components/best-slips-route.tsx), [Browse actions](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips-browse-presentation.tsx), [Navigation progress](../next-js/src/components/navigation/navigation-progress.tsx).

Every 60 seconds, a visible, online and idle finite-period view checks one loaded page, rotating through appended pages. With several pages open, a passive read updates matching slips and period totals while preserving the accepted list membership and order. A transient failure keeps query-compatible rows and counts and labels them as last confirmed. A failed filter change offers a retry for the requested query and keeps the accepted filters visible. An unavailable initial server read gets one delayed client attempt while visible and online. Passive reads pause while hidden or offline and after a failure. A suitable reconnection or visibility transition can revalidate retained results. [Browse acceptance and retry](../next-js/src/components/slips/use-best-slips-browse.ts), [Observation merge](../next-js/src/domain/slip-browse-window.ts), [Empty and unavailable states](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips.tsx).

The API reads the viewer-independent archive once and confirms access immediately before projecting the response. A bounded cache can reuse decoded archived forecast evidence after the current transaction rechecks the byte seals and binding fields. Fixture, cycle and result data stay live. The archive transaction allows up to five seconds to acquire a connection and up to 30 seconds for the read. It establishes a repeatable-read snapshot before sampling database time. Invalid criteria return HTTP 400. An unavailable archive or access check returns HTTP 503. [Archive read](../next-js/src/server/slips/published-slips.ts), [Forecast evidence](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-archive-revisions.ts), [HTTP status handling](../next-js/src/server/slips/best-slips-http.ts).

Editorial result reads reuse previously verified original goal forecasts for slips, counts and additional pages. Each application process retains up to 1,024 compact projections within an 8 MiB serialized budget. The current database snapshot still confirms that the row exists, checks its body seal, and matches the revision, fixture, cycle, timestamp, rule version and content hash. A new or changed seal loads the full body and rederives the goal evidence. Raw request timings distinguish archive headers, rows and forecast evidence. [Goal evidence reader](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-goal-forecast.ts), [Canonical goal evidence](../next-js/src/server/predictions/goal-market-store.ts), [Request timing](../next-js/src/server/http/request-timing.ts).

Full-access desktop browsing also refreshes the visible slips' original-selection states in one batched `POST /api/slips/match-states`, normally every 20 seconds. The shared observation state updates status, score and clock for each selected fixture and keeps the published picks, odds and disclosure controls in place. The compact list uses the states from the browse response. The desktop status badge can display observed added time and paused phases. The batched loop pauses when hidden or offline, backs off after transient failures and stops on access withdrawal. [Slip list](../next-js/src/components/slips/best-slips.tsx), [Shared observations](../next-js/src/components/slips/slip-match-refresh.tsx), [Observation request rules](../next-js/src/domain/slip-match-refresh.ts), [Polling and backoff](../next-js/src/domain/slip-match-polling.ts), [Desktop fixture row](../next-js/src/components/slips/desktop-slip/desktop-slip-row.tsx).

### Access, settlement and sharing

Public summaries include total odds, arithmetic average leg probability and any originally stored joint estimate. Guests can see safe fixture previews and verified completed legs. The full card can also be released after verified completion of the original slip. The reader applies current access before returning unfinished selections. A 401 or 403 on browse or observation refresh clears private selections and triggers an access recheck. Live play, elapsed kickoff, schedule changes and trusted early-play evidence can make a slip unavailable for placement. Displayed statuses and settled outcomes use the original cycle and revision. Cancelled or postponed occurrences can become void according to recorded cycle evidence. The original total remains the archived price. [Access projection](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-preview.ts), [Forecast and results](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-forecast.ts), [Slip status](../next-js/src/domain/slip-status.ts), [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md).

Public links use `/en/best-slips/{day}/{rank}`. The route validates the reporting date and public rank, reads the audited original publication and rechecks access and completion. Share resolves the selected publication by its stored slip identity and applies the same result and visibility rules. When image preparation or native sharing fails, the drawer offers to copy the canonical link if it has already been established. [Public rank route](../next-js/src/app/_components/public-best-slip-route.tsx), [Publication reader](../next-js/src/server/slips/published-slips.ts), [Share handler](../next-js/src/server/slips/shared-slips-http.ts), [Slip sharing](../next-js/docs/slip-sharing.md).

## Verification and limits

Focused tests cover the operational window, probability and span order, exact odds boundaries, cross-band priority, concurrent allocation, append-only history and access. Current worker code stores no qualified whole-slip estimate for new batches. It has no bookmaker payout feed to calculate effective settled odds after a void. Coverage, bookmaker availability and the running hosted schedule depend on the target environment. [Ranking tests](../next-js/tests/automatic-slip-ranking.test.mjs), [Slot and band tests](../next-js/tests/best-slip-odds-bands.test.mjs), [Publication integration tests](../next-js/tests/published-slips.integration.mjs), [November 27 baseline and replay](../next-js/docs/reports/best-slips-canonical-selection-2026-11-27.md), [Deployment](deployment-and-version-control.md).

The 27 November production release verified the web and publisher at `68436794`. The first paired slot committed two same-book slips in the 2.50–5.00 band with 15- and 30-minute kickoff spans. The other five bands had no retained eligible alternatives in that slot. The read-only comparison preserved the fingerprints of the pre-promotion archive. [Production promotion](deployment-and-version-control.md#distant-carryover-production-release), [Slot and archive verification](../next-js/docs/reports/best-slips-canonical-selection-2026-11-27.md#production-promotion).

For the later 27 November web release at `88ea84ce`, isolated rendering tests exercised populated lists, cursor continuation, retry, stable rows and browser history. Hosted staging returned HTTP 200 for all six band requests.
