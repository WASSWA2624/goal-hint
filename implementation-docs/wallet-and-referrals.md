# Wallet and referral rewards

Wallet credit funds subscription plans in whole Uganda shillings. Earned referral days are a separate balance that members can redeem for access time. Wallet and checkout require a current session for an admitted, unblocked ordinary member. Referrals and appeals require phone verification. [Account workspace](authentication.md#account-workspace-and-notices), [payments](payments.md), [subscription access](subscriptions.md).

## Wallet balance, funding and spending

- **Account → Wallet** shows the recorded balance, a separate available amount when reservations reduce it, a debt notice when negative, top-up status, auto-renewal, and up to 20 recent ledger entries. History shows each amount, resulting balance, date, and any linked payment or access reference. Wallet credit is non-refundable and has no withdrawal action. [Wallet UI](../next-js/src/components/members/account-wallet.tsx), [member payment and wallet view](../next-js/src/server/members/payments.ts), [wallet ledger](../next-js/src/server/members/wallet.ts).
- **Top up** opens the dedicated manual Mobile Money flow when new checkout is available and at least one receiving account is configured. The member requests UGX 1,000–500,000 in whole shillings. The current workflow waits for verified receipts covering the requested amount. Approval appends the requested amount as a top-up and records additional verified funds separately as overpayment. Partial receipts leave the request waiting for the remainder. Approval creates no access period. Flutterwave funds plan checkout only. [Top-up entry](../next-js/src/domain/account-navigation.ts), [amount rules](../next-js/src/domain/wallet.ts), [receipt allocation](../next-js/src/server/members/checkout.ts).
- In plan checkout, the member can apply spendable credit up to the package price. Part funding reduces the transfer amount, and full funding completes after review and confirmation without administrator approval. An open manual request can hold the selected wallet credit and referral days for 48 hours. Cancellation or expiry releases the hold, and verified receipts stay recorded for reconciliation. Expiry moves a request to funding reconfirmation or keeps its dispute open. Reconfirmation checks both available balances and the payable amount before taking a fresh hold. Verified plan approval commits the debit, any excess credit and the access period together. The checkout enforces a UGX 2,000,000,000 integer limit on added credit and the resulting balance. [Checkout selections and limits](../next-js/src/domain/checkout.ts), [checkout service](../next-js/src/server/members/checkout.ts), [reservation and receipt tests](../next-js/tests/checkout.integration.mjs).
- The append-only `WalletEntry` ledger records the signed amount and resulting balance. The account lock serializes spending and updates `MemberAccount.creditUgx` in the same transaction. Entry sequencing and request keys protect retries. Purchases and renewals respect other active reservations. The existing `/api/account/credit` purchase action also buys an active plan immediately using an account-scoped request key and current terms. [Wallet mutation](../next-js/src/server/members/wallet.ts), [credit endpoint](../next-js/src/app/api/account/credit/route.ts), [wallet integration tests](../next-js/tests/wallet.integration.mjs).
- **Auto-renewal** is opt-in. First enrollment needs positive spendable credit, current terms and an active plan while new checkout is available. A due renewal uses the selected plan’s current price, rechecks the account, paid-access switch and spendable credit, and buys at most once per recorded period end. The lead is one hour. The worker handles lapses up to one day old, while a member visit can trigger a later due renewal. A trial alone does not charge the wallet. Members can turn renewal off even when sales are paused. [Renewal service](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [scheduled renewal sweep](../next-js/src/server/members/auto-renew.ts), [renewal and access rules](subscriptions.md#end-to-end-flows).

## Wallet corrections, transfers and reversals

- **Admin → Users → Credit** shows the selected account’s balance and up to 100 ledger entries. Authorized administrators can turn off renewal and use **Correct credit** to confirm a signed nonzero correction up to UGX 500,000 in magnitude. The reason is recorded in the ledger and shown in credit history. A debit correction requires enough balance. An audited user number transfer moves the wallet balance, including debt, through paired ledger entries and can carry the renewal setting. Open payment requests and unapplied receipts can block the transfer. Member self-service phone changes retain the same account. [Credit tab](../next-js/src/components/admin/user-detail.tsx), [user actions](../next-js/src/server/admin/users-http.ts), [credit correction and transfer](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts).
- Legacy payment reversal recalls the top-up or excess credit, follows later audited transfers to its current holder, and can record a negative balance when credit was already spent. Debt prevents new purchases and renewals until sufficient credit is restored. Where a legacy payment granted access, reversal revokes the linked surviving period and reschedules remaining access. The legacy action refuses version-two checkout allocations, which use the receipt reconciliation and ownership workflow. [Reversal rules](../next-js/src/server/members/access-codes.ts), [receipt reconciliation](payments.md).
- Version-two allocation also fails its wallet reservation check when the recorded balance is negative and the request reserves zero wallet credit. A top-up or earned-day redemption on that path can reach reconfirmation while the debt remains. [Allocation guard](../next-js/src/server/members/checkout.ts).
- Account erasure forfeits a positive wallet balance through a ledger adjustment, retains debt and accounting entries, and turns off renewal. Permanent deletion is limited by open requests, cash history and wallet ledger dependencies. [User erasure](../next-js/src/server/admin/users.ts), [deletion checks](../next-js/src/server/admin/delete-user.ts).

## Invitations and reward eligibility

- New accounts receive a random eight-digit code with leading zeros preserved. Legacy 32-character hexadecimal codes remain valid aliases. An unblocked, phone-verified member can view their personal link in Account → Referrals and use **Copy link**, WhatsApp, Telegram or supported device sharing. **Invite friends** uses the public site link when a verified personal invitation is unavailable. The account summary shows verified referrals, days earned, spendable days and legacy remaining reward access. Recent history shows up to ten registrations with status and time. Invitee contact details and phone digests stay private. [Invitation UI](../next-js/src/components/members/account-referrals.tsx), [navigation invitation](../next-js/src/components/navigation/session-navigation.tsx), [code and history service](../next-js/src/server/members/referrals.ts).
- `/r/[code]` redirects to Signup. The first eligible document visit sets a signed HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax attribution cookie for 30 days, with the `__Host-` prefix and Secure flag on production. Prefetches and embedded requests skip attribution. An existing valid cookie keeps its original expiry and inviter. Invalid, expired or unavailable attribution still opens ordinary Signup. Successful signup clears the cookie and records the phone’s registration identity if its keyed digest is new. Existing accounts cannot attach an inviter later. Digests retained after erasure or deletion prevent repeat awards. [Referral route and signature](../next-js/src/server/members/referral-attribution.ts), [signup binding](../next-js/src/server/members/member-auth-http.ts), [referral identity tests](../next-js/tests/referrals.integration.mjs).
- The eligible inviter must be a distinct, phone-verified, unblocked, unerased ordinary member with a password. The app rechecks both accounts and the original registration phone when awarding. The eligible legacy account-code verification path commits one `ReferralCredit` day and changes the registration from pending to rewarded in the same transaction. A previously used phone identity or invalid inviter can leave the registration ineligible. Rewards require no purchase and have no configured lifetime cap or unused balance expiry. [Award service](../next-js/src/server/members/referrals.ts), [account-code verification](../next-js/src/server/members/member-auth.ts).
- **Current verification gap.** Signup sends no verification code. Manual password recovery can establish phone proof and currently does not call the award service. An attributed registration can therefore remain pending even after that recovery flow verifies the invitee. Operator code provisioning creates or converts links only. [Recovery transaction](../next-js/src/server/members/password-recovery.ts), [operator provisioning](../next-js/src/workers/referrals.ts).

## Redeeming earned days

**Redeem days** appears for verified members with a spendable reward balance while Paid Access is On. The available-days count subtracts days held by open checkout requests. The member chooses one to 365 available days in the separate checkout review and confirms the access period. Days are deducted from the referral ledger when the period is issued. Access starts at the latest unrevoked expiry, or immediately if it has passed. Existing compatible manual or wallet plan requests can retain referral days as a duration extension. The days affect duration only, and the quoted cash price remains the plan price less selected wallet funding. An audited number transfer moves unused ledger days and remaining legacy earned periods while preserving original award attribution. [Account referral controls](../next-js/src/components/members/account-referrals.tsx), [balance calculation](../next-js/src/server/members/referrals.ts), [allocation and period creation](../next-js/src/server/members/checkout.ts), [reward scheduling](../next-js/src/server/members/referral-periods.ts).

## Referral review and appeals

- **Admin → Referral review** searches cases by status, phone or reference and can open a case from an earned grant. The administrator selects a controlled reason and records a bounded summary and evidence reference. Confirmation requires checked evidence and revokes only unused milliseconds of the linked earned grant. Already consumed or already reversed time yields no additional reversal. The member sees controlled reasons and the outcome in Referrals, can appeal a reversal once, and receives private in-app notices. The administrator can uphold the decision or restore the exact reversed time through one compensating grant. The current holder owns the appeal after an audited transfer. [Admin review](../next-js/src/components/admin/referral-reviews-editor.tsx), [member appeal](../next-js/src/components/members/account-referral-reviews.tsx), [review service](../next-js/src/server/members/referral-reviews.ts).
- The review service targets `AccessCode` records with `source = referral` and a linked original award. Unspent `ReferralCredit` and access issued through current checkout redemption are outside that review path. Account locks, case revisions, and a unique grant reversal record protect against repeated deductions. Supplementary evidence is private and becomes eligible for cleanup 90 days after closure. Maintenance processes up to 250 overdue cases per run, and erasure scrubs the affected evidence. Controlled decisions and accounting history remain. [Review rules](../next-js/src/domain/referral-reviews.ts), [review integration tests](../next-js/tests/referral-reviews.integration.mjs), [member notices](authentication.md#account-workspace-and-notices).
