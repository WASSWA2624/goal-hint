# Odds and market updates

Goal Hint pulls bookmaker offers from API-Football through a private worker. The worker also refreshes the provider’s market and bookmaker catalogs. It validates offers against stored fixture identities and writes them to MySQL or MariaDB. Matches, Match Details and slips read eligible prices from storage. Published forecast probabilities follow their own workflow and can remain available when an exact price is missing. [Odds adapter](../next-js/src/server/football/api-football-adapter.ts), [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Markets and display](markets-and-display.md), [Prediction engine](prediction-engine.md).

## Collection schedule

The table documents source schedules. The observations below record the prior and new production workers.

| Work | Normal cadence | Fetch and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-match catalogs | Once per UTC provider day | The worker queues `/odds/bets` and `/odds/bookmakers` ahead of ordinary price sweeps. The bet catalog supplies the IDs and names used to verify and map the offers. |
| Broad pre-match price sweep | Every three hours by default, starting two minutes after the UTC slot | Durable jobs call paginated `/odds?date=YYYY-MM-DD&timezone=Africa/Kampala`, one job per Kampala date. The base schedule covers today and up to six days ahead. The authorized recovery configuration extends fixture discovery and odds collection through day 14. Coordinated production uses all returned bookmakers, markets and lines within its request limits. |
| Selective odds reconciliation | Every 30 minutes by default, at `:05` and `:35` Kampala time | The same enrichment worker selects up to 24 upcoming fixtures per attempt for `/odds?fixture=…`. It prioritizes approaching kickoffs, missing odds, incomplete markets and stale near-kickoff quotes. Recent qualifying broad and fixture receipts suppress repeated checks. Transient failures can retry within the slot, and confirmed empty responses become eligible again later. |
| Targeted Best Slips refresh | Optional, at `:25` and `:55` Kampala time ahead of each half-hour publication slot | A higher-priority job selects up to 12 fixtures with a current forecast and no eligible price. Kickoff must be at least 30 minutes away and within six hours of the slot. The worker calls `/odds?fixture=…` once per selected fixture, prioritizing fixtures never checked or checked longest ago. A verified empty response counts as a check. With the switch enabled on both runtimes, the publisher waits for this slot’s job to succeed and can wait up to five minutes after the slot. |
| Historical pre-match recovery | Eligible from 30 minutes after a broad slot when capacity permits | Separate jobs cover the previous seven Kampala dates. They prioritize dates nearing expiry and reuse verified progress across UTC quota days. Recovery stores available observations with their actual retrieval and provider clocks. |
| In-play catalog | Due 24 hours after a successful refresh, with a 15-minute retry after an unsuccessful check | `/odds/live/bets` supplies a separate ID and name catalog for live markets. Catalog checks run when live odds collection has eligible work. |
| In-play prices | At most one shared price request per 60 seconds when eligible | A database fence reserves a shared `/odds/live` poll. Eligible stored fixtures include live play, scheduled fixtures from 45 minutes past kickoff to 15 minutes before kickoff, and recent finals or abandonments within 20 minutes of the recorded final observation. The hosted path scopes retained offers to configured competitions. |

[Pre-match schedules](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-jobs.ts), [Selective reconciliation](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-reconciliation.ts), [Targeted publication slot](../next-js/src/server/odds/slip-odds-refresh-slot.ts), [Publisher gate](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-publication-command.ts), [In-play collector](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-service.ts).

The worker schedules batches independently of page traffic. `GOAL_HINT_ODDS_IMPORT_ENABLED` defaults off. Imports require a configured primary bookmaker, verified policy and eligible provider capacity with standard pacing of at least 60 requests per minute and at least 200 spare daily requests in the plan calculation. The dedicated enrichment worker shares the durable quota account and preserves reserves for fixtures, results, forecasts and live details. A broad date sweep can request up to 150 pages per attempt and continue from sealed pages within the slot, subject to the 1,000-page chain limit and quota. Capacity pressure can double the interval. Fixture-specific checks can wait out a transient local pacing or priority delay within their eight-second deadline, with one physical provider call per fixture. The optional Best Slips refresh also needs `GOAL_HINT_SLIP_ODDS_REFRESH_ENABLED` and verified odds display rights. [Plan and quota limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Job scheduling and capacity](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runner.ts), [Runtime gates](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

API-Football documents a three-hour upstream pre-match update, a seven-day pre-match history window and variable in-play updates of about 5–60 seconds. The app’s polling intervals and freshness rules are separate from those upstream timings. Past in-play prices and older pre-match prices depend on earlier authorized capture. [API-Football v3 documentation](https://www.api-football.com/documentation-v3).

## Validation, storage and freshness

`OddsImportBatch` records import provenance and `ProviderOddsSnapshot` retains normalized pre-match price history. `FixtureOddsState` tracks the latest accepted scope for each fixture, and `FixtureOddsPrice` stores the public price projection. Recovered pre-match and in-play observations have their own tables. The worker retains structured odds data under its retention authority. Public display has a separate approval. [Database schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Odds store](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-mysql-store.ts), [Runtime authority](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

- **Pre-match imports** match the provider fixture ID and kickoff to the stored occurrence and retain structured bookmaker, market, line and selection records. Each verified page and its snapshots are committed together. Retries resume at the first unfinished page. Partial outcomes fail the job with a reason, and inconsistent page sets are prevented from withdrawing unseen prices. A complete nonempty date sweep can withdraw missing offers. Verified empty fixture and date observations retain the previously accepted prices at their original clocks. A current quote needs reconfirmation within 12 hours and an eligible provider update clock when one is supplied. Closing prices freeze qualifying pre-kickoff quotes. Recovered prices keep separate provenance and actual clocks. [Import service](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-service.ts), [Odds store](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-mysql-store.ts), [Freshness contract](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-contract.ts).
- **In-play imports** use a separate market namespace and are attributed to the API-Football live source. Observations are checked against the fixture, teams, competition, season and kickoff. Blocked or suspended offers are withheld. A complete listing can withdraw an absent offer, while an incomplete market leaves the last confirmed offer at its original clock. The current projection is available while the fixture is live and expires after 90 seconds without fresh confirmation, including a freshness check on the provider clock when present. Authorized structured history retains changes and extends the observed time range of repeated identical offers. [In-play collector](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-service.ts), [In-play history](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-history.ts), [In-play reads](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-read.ts).
- **Market matching** checks the catalog and the exact period, market, team or player where applicable, line and selection. Unmapped or unsupported provider entries can be retained with a reason. Eligible reference pricing uses the preferred bookmaker, configured fallbacks and then an eligible exact offer when the read allows it. A read for a specific bookmaker stays pinned to that bookmaker. [Market registry](../next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [Pre-match mapping](../next-js/src/domain/odds-markets.ts), [Reference selection](../next-js/src/domain/reference-odds.ts).

Selective reports distinguish stored odds, missing markets and selections, stale near-kickoff quotes, recent provider empties, and failed or deferred checks. Reports include quote age and backlog. The eligible provider-offer denominator and the 99% capture target require a production measurement that compares verified provider responses with persisted rows. [Selective coverage](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-reconciliation.ts).

## Delivery to Matches, Match Details and slips

Public reads verify the configured odds display approval and apply viewer access rules. In Matches, an odds filter uses the stored two-decimal filter price for the exact pick that would be shown. The eligible price affects membership before the list count and pagination. Both desktop rows and compact cards suppress decimal prices. Eligible observations and featured choices can update with the ordinary stored-data refresh, normally every 20 seconds during active play or near kickoff and every 60 seconds in quiet views. [Display rights](../next-js/src/server/odds/public-odds-policy.ts), [Viewer access](../next-js/src/domain/paywall.ts), [Feed odds filters](../next-js/src/server/matches/feed-read.ts), [Feed quote reads](../next-js/src/server/matches/feed-odds.ts), [Fixtures and live data](fixtures-and-live-data.md#what-the-browser-refreshes).

In the applicable Match Details view, **Markets & Odds** can show current pre-match comparisons before kickoff, in-play prices while live, closing prices and qualifying recovered pre-match observations as separate sections. These stored observations can refresh independently of the forecast. Inspecting an explicit revision or cycle keeps its archived forecast evidence and hides the current bookmaker panels. The desktop sidebar and compact tabs share those panels. A provider market can have eligible odds without a published forecast. [Detail reads](../next-js/src/server/matches/detail-service.ts), [Match Details quote panels](../next-js/src/components/match/details/touchline-tabs.tsx), [UI implementation](ui-implementation.md).

The bookmaker comparison orders the configured primary bookmaker first when its eligible prices fit in the response, then other books by ID. Its selector changes which book’s exact offers are shown. A market with more than 12 visible prices uses **Show prices**. A suspended live panel reports suspension. Large comparisons can omit markets to stay within the response budget and show a limit notice. Recovered selections need a stored observation to enter the comparison. They retain their retrieval and check times, with the provider’s update time when supplied. [Bookmaker projection](../next-js/src/server/odds/bookmaker-odds-read.ts), [Bookmaker controls](../next-js/src/components/match/details/in-play-odds.tsx).

The current featured decision counts distinct verified bookmakers offering the exact selection. Eligibility includes the same occurrence and kickoff, market name and ID, outcome label, usable price and fresh observation. A repeated bookmaker ID does not add support. The count is a tie-break within the applicable probability and pricing tier. One verified offer can qualify by itself. Builder reads eligible exact quotes for its user-selected outcomes. Automatic Best Slips keep each published slip on one bookmaker and seal the selected odds at publication. [Featured quote checks](../next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [Builder price reads](../next-js/src/server/slips/price-read.ts), [Slip Builder](slip-builder.md), [Automatic Slips](automatic-slips.md).
