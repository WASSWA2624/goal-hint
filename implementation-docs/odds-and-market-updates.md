# Odds and market updates

Goal Hint pulls bookmaker offers from API-Football through a private worker. The worker also refreshes the provider’s market and bookmaker catalogs. It validates offers against stored fixture identities and writes them to MySQL or MariaDB. Matches, Match Details and slips read eligible prices from storage. Published forecast probabilities follow their own workflow and can remain available when an exact price is missing. [Odds adapter](../next-js/src/server/football/api-football-adapter.ts), [Worker runtime](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [Markets and display](markets-and-display.md), [Prediction engine](prediction-engine.md).

## Collection schedule

| Work | Normal cadence | Fetch and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pre-match catalogs | Once per UTC provider day | The worker queues `/odds/bets` and `/odds/bookmakers` ahead of ordinary price sweeps. The bet catalog supplies the IDs and names used to verify and map the offers. |
| Broad pre-match price sweep | Every three hours, aligned to UTC (also on the three-hour marks in Kampala) | Durable jobs call paginated `/odds?date=YYYY-MM-DD&timezone=Africa/Kampala`, one job per Kampala date and nearest dates first. The base schedule covers today and up to six days ahead. The authorized recovery configuration extends it through day 14. The basic configuration can filter by the primary bookmaker. Coordinated production uses all returned bookmakers, markets and lines within its request limits. |
| Targeted Best Slips refresh | Optional, at `:25` and `:55` Kampala time ahead of each half-hour publication slot | A higher-priority job selects up to 12 fixtures with a current forecast and no eligible price. Kickoff must be at least 30 minutes away and within six hours of the slot. The worker calls `/odds?fixture=…` once per selected fixture, prioritizing fixtures never checked or checked longest ago. A verified empty response counts as a check. With the switch enabled on both runtimes, the publisher waits for this slot’s job to succeed and can wait up to five minutes after the slot. |
| Historical pre-match recovery | Scheduled alongside an eligible recovery-enabled broad sweep | Separate jobs cover the previous seven Kampala dates. They prioritize dates nearing expiry and reuse verified progress across UTC quota days. Recovery stores available observations with their actual retrieval and provider clocks. |
| In-play catalog | Due 24 hours after a successful refresh, with a 15-minute retry after an unsuccessful check | `/odds/live/bets` supplies a separate ID and name catalog for live markets. Catalog checks run when live odds collection has eligible work. |
| In-play prices | At most one shared price request per 60 seconds when eligible | A database fence reserves a shared `/odds/live` poll. Eligible stored fixtures include live play, scheduled fixtures from 45 minutes past kickoff to 15 minutes before kickoff, and recent finals or abandonments within 20 minutes of the recorded final observation. The hosted path scopes retained offers to configured competitions. |

[Pre-match and recovery schedules](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-jobs.ts), [Targeted slot](../next-js/src/server/odds/slip-odds-refresh-slot.ts), [Candidate selection](../next-js/src/server/odds/slip-odds-refresh-candidates.ts), [Publisher gate](../next-js/src/server/slips/slip-publication-command.ts), [In-play collector](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-service.ts).

The worker schedules batches independently of page traffic. `GOAL_HINT_ODDS_IMPORT_ENABLED` defaults off. Imports require a configured primary bookmaker, verified policy and eligible provider capacity with standard pacing of at least 60 requests per minute and at least 200 spare daily requests in the plan calculation. The dedicated enrichment worker shares the durable quota account and preserves reserves for fixtures, results, forecasts and live details. A broad sweep can request up to 150 pages per date, subject to quota, deadlines and response limits. Under capacity pressure, the broad schedule can stretch to six hours and live work can be delayed. The optional targeted path also needs `GOAL_HINT_SLIP_ODDS_REFRESH_ENABLED` and verified odds display rights. [Plan and quota limits](../next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [Job scheduling and capacity](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runner.ts), [Runtime gates](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

API-Football documents a three-hour upstream pre-match update, a seven-day pre-match history window and variable in-play updates of about 5–60 seconds. The app’s polling intervals and freshness rules are separate from those upstream timings. Past in-play prices and older pre-match prices depend on earlier authorized capture. [API-Football v3 documentation](https://www.api-football.com/documentation-v3).

## Validation, storage and freshness

`OddsImportBatch` records import provenance and `ProviderOddsSnapshot` retains normalized pre-match price history. `FixtureOddsState` tracks the latest accepted scope for each fixture, and `FixtureOddsPrice` stores the public price projection. Recovered pre-match and in-play observations have their own tables. The worker retains structured odds data under its retention authority. Public display has a separate approval. [Database schema](../next-js/prisma/schema.prisma), [Odds store](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-mysql-store.ts), [Runtime authority](../next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

- **Pre-match imports** match the provider fixture ID and kickoff to the stored occurrence and retain structured bookmaker, market, line and selection records. In resumable mode, each verified page and its snapshots are committed together so a retry resumes at the first unfinished page. A complete date sweep can withdraw a missing offer. Partial sweeps preserve unseen fixtures, and an empty targeted response applies only to its fixture scope. A current quote needs reconfirmation within 12 hours and an eligible provider update clock when one is supplied. Closing prices freeze qualifying pre-kickoff quotes. Recovered prices have separate provenance and keep their actual clocks. [Import service](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-service.ts), [Odds store](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-mysql-store.ts), [Freshness contract](../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-contract.ts).
- **In-play imports** use a separate market namespace and are attributed to the API-Football live source. Observations are checked against the fixture, teams, competition, season and kickoff. Blocked or suspended offers are withheld. A complete listing can withdraw an absent offer, while an incomplete market leaves the last confirmed offer at its original clock. The current projection is available while the fixture is live and expires after 90 seconds without fresh confirmation, including a freshness check on the provider clock when present. Authorized structured history retains changes and extends the observed time range of repeated identical offers. [In-play collector](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-service.ts), [In-play history](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-history.ts), [In-play reads](../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-read.ts).
- **Market matching** checks the catalog and the exact period, market, team or player where applicable, line and selection. Unmapped or unsupported provider entries can be retained with a reason. Eligible reference pricing uses the preferred bookmaker, configured fallbacks and then an eligible exact offer when the read allows it. A read for a specific bookmaker stays pinned to that bookmaker. [Market registry](../next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [Pre-match mapping](../next-js/src/domain/odds-markets.ts), [Reference selection](../next-js/src/domain/reference-odds.ts).

## Delivery to Matches, Match Details and slips

Public reads verify the configured odds display approval and apply viewer access rules. Matches uses eligible stored quotes for filters and pick decisions, while its feed cards suppress decimal prices. Match Details can show distinct pre-match, in-play, closing and recovered offers. A verified provider-only market can appear as a quote without a published forecast. Eligible current quotes can update with the ordinary Matches and Match Details polling, normally every 20 seconds during active play or near kickoff and every 60 seconds in quiet views. The browser reads Goal Hint’s stored data endpoints. [Display rights](../next-js/src/server/odds/public-odds-policy.ts), [Viewer access](../next-js/src/domain/paywall.ts), [Feed quote reads](../next-js/src/server/matches/feed-odds.ts), [Match Details quote panels](../next-js/src/components/match/details/touchline-tabs.tsx), [Fixtures and live data](fixtures-and-live-data.md#what-the-browser-refreshes).

Custom Builder can use a fresh exact quote from any eligible verified bookmaker for a supported published outcome. Best Slips uses one bookmaker per slip and seals its selected odds at publication. Current refreshes leave those publication terms intact. [Builder price reads](../next-js/src/server/slips/price-read.ts), [Slip Builder](slip-builder.md), [Automatic Slips](automatic-slips.md).
