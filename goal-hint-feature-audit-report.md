## Key risks

- **Local verification does not establish remote deployment.** Eight pending local migrations applied after a private backup and isolated restore drill. New-table grants are verified without application DDL privileges. Remote production schema, workers, messages and handset behavior were not inspected.
- **Referral identities need the stable private key.** Retained keyed digests prevent replay after erasure/deletion, including verified replacement numbers. Previously erased data cannot be retrospectively matched; key rotation needs an identity migration. Shared IPs are not proof of duplicate people; broader fraud investigation remains separate.
- **Earned time is access time.** Each award is 24 hours and runs continuously once scheduled after paid/trial access. It is not a daily login bonus or money balance. Unused time has no expiry; paid purchases preserve exact remaining milliseconds; credit renewal waits until earned access ends.
- **Sessions, paid-device grants and security changes differ.** Two account sessions may coexist; paid access has an independent device cap. A signed-in password change uses the authenticated session, keeps a fresh current-device cookie and revokes every previous session. Signed-out recovery still verifies phone ownership.
- **Sharing promises exceed recipient access.** Public-link wording promises anyone can view selections while active selections require entitlement. Align copy/landing without weakening the gate. Distributed images cannot be revoked.
- **Provider/delivery confidence remains limited.** Proxy trust, message receipt, provider truth, evaluation approval and release signing remain unverified. Synthetic UI metrics are not production quality or personal betting results.

## Dependencies and unanswered questions

- **Resolved Custom Filters scope:** Slip Builder only; staged selection plus explicit Use; save times but use today in Africa/Kampala; blank times mean 00:00–23:59; optional default fills a fresh visit with interaction/query safeguards. No new design decision is needed.
- **Resolved referral/access policy:** first valid link for 30 days; immutable new-account attribution; award at first eligible phone verification regardless of payment; self/existing/duplicate/ineligible claims rejected; unlimited valid accumulation; paid/queued/trial → earned → credit renewal. Exact unused rewards survive paid purchases and number transfers; ordinary phone changes retain the account/balance. See [rules](next-js/docs/referral-rewards.md).
- **Resolved password/session policy:** no current password or OTP for authenticated password changes; no signed-in Forgot password; fresh current-device cookie and all old sessions revoked. Phone changes retain current-password/OTP verification. Members/admins share the two-session login cap; routine new-device login retains phone/password authentication.
- **Remaining decisions:** proportionate fraud review/reversal policy, relevant notification/result events, active-share recipient wording/landing, individual-prediction sharing, player-data coverage/rights, approved evaluation policy and reporting windows. Remote release/worker/physical-device qualification remains necessary.
