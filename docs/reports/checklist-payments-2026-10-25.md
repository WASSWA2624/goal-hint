## Official-source review

- Yoola's [privacy policy](https://yoolasms.com/privacy-policy) describes collecting recipient numbers, message content and timestamps, telecom delivery disclosures, authorized production access, twelve-month message logs and account-closure deletion subject to legal retention. Its [security page](https://yoolasms.com/security) instead states a default maximum of ninety days for message content, identifies Africa's Talking as a delivery partner and offers an enterprise DPA. These published statements conflict on retention; neither establishes Goal Hint's data locations or contract. Record the applicable written answer before promising a gateway deletion period.
- Yoola's [terms](https://yoolasms.com/terms) require lawful, consented messaging, prohibit misleading content, reserve monitoring for compliance and require sender-ID approval. Used message credits generally are not refunded merely because delivery fails. The adapter's provider-acceptance wording and no automatic retries remain appropriate; no test here sends a real SMS.
- Uganda's official [Data Protection and Privacy Act, Cap. 97](https://nita.go.ug/sites/default/files/2026-09/Data%20Protection%20and%20Privacy%20Act%20cap%2097.pdf) covers purpose/minimality, retention, processing abroad, security and a processing register (sections 12–14, 18–23 and 29). [PDPO registration guidance](https://pdpo.go.ug/media//2022/01/20102021105143-Registration_Classification_and_Guidance_Notes.pdf) describes registration of collectors/controllers/processors. Actual registration, lawful bases and processing arrangements were not evidenced by the repository.
- The official NLGRB publishes the [governing Acts](https://lgrb.go.ug/lgrb-acts/), [betting/licensing regulations](https://lgrb.go.ug/regulations/) and [advertising guidelines](https://lgrb.go.ug/download/nlgrb-advertising-guidelines/). Their existence is verified. The advertising attachment did not fetch successfully in this review; detailed applicability to paid prediction information, marketing, affiliate activity and the operator's actual model remains unresolved. No licensing exemption or approval is inferred.

## Corrections and verification

The screenshot was required at three layers despite existing optional-screenshot documentation. It is now optional in the visible form, multipart boundary and storage service. A supplied image remains validated, reports stay pending for manual receipt verification, and reports without images consume the existing submission count limits. A screenshot-derived amount is discarded when no image accompanies the report.

Financial and subscription data was included in outgoing payment/credit/renewal SMS. All use a short generic account notice now. Help/privacy content and subscription/Yoola documentation describe the new behavior.

- Passed: 32 payment HTTP, consent, notification, concurrency/replay and no-image service tests; 16 Yoola adapter tests had passed in the initial focused run (48 distinct tests total).
- Passed: 20 access-period and paywall checks.
- Skipped: 3 MySQL integration suites (payment transactions, owner review, standard-package migration): no genuine `mysqld` binary is available. Existing MariaDB/services are deliberately not reused as disposable integration targets.
- Passed after serializing builds and pinning Node 24.18.1: `verify-payments-rendering.py --fixture .tmp/payments-a6454fa9 --serve`, covering 19 server-rendered payment states, optional screenshot labels/controls, required transaction ID/payer, credit flows, expiry, private headers and five user-agent variants. Browser interaction is separate from these HTML assertions. Repository-wide lint/typecheck/build are coordinated by the primary agent.

No database migration is required for these corrections. No payment decision or SMS send was performed outside synthetic tests.
