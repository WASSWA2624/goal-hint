## Lifecycle at a glance

1. A private worker imports and validates provider observations, resolves canonical competition, season, team and fixture identities, and reconciles the schedule. Stored odds and result observations have separate lifecycles.
2. A daily or hourly run freezes the eligible fixture membership and queues bounded refresh work. A refresh pins its inputs, collects evidence, captures an analysis cutoff, and evaluates the applicable source policy.
3. Valid distributions produce market forecasts and may also produce a recommendation. The worker checks current fixture status before publishing. Atomic publication appends a complete revision and moves the open cycle's current pointer.
4. The cutoff seals the eligible prediction revision for the occurrence and can freeze its featured pick. A result worker confirms what happened. Settlement grades the frozen identity against verified result evidence and records any corrections.
5. Matches, Match Details, Performance and slips read projections of these records. Historical reconstruction and evaluation have separate data and authorization paths.

| Term                                     | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixture and occurrence                   | A canonical fixture maps to a provider identity. The occurrence is the particular schedule and participant context being forecast. Its prediction cycle can change after postponement or reconciliation.                                                       |
| Run, job and intent                      | A run freezes fixture membership and operating policy. A durable job executes one member's refresh. The intent records which configured policies, model and resource bounds that job may use.                                                                  |
| Analysis cutoff                          | The point-in-time ceiling on inputs to a forecast. Evidence and quotes have their own source and observation clocks.                                                                                                                                           |
| Publication deadline and effective close | The normal write deadline is scheduled kickoff minus five minutes. Observed early play can make the valid cutoff earlier.                                                                                                                                      |
| Market, selection and forecast           | A market defines a football question with an exact period, subject and, where relevant, line. A selection is one answer. A forecast identifies the source and estimated probability distribution for a supported group.                                        |
| Recommendation and featured pick         | A recommendation is a decision stored with an eligible forecast. The released Local Engine featured policy can choose a different exact selection for default display and freeze it at cutoff. Explicit market filters have their own visible-pick projection. |
| Result and settlement                    | A result is versioned evidence about the fixture. A settlement is the grade for the exact selection and rules of a locked prediction.                                                                                                                          |

## Fixture intake, selection and refresh

**Import and reconcile.** The private API-Football adapter bounds time, requests, pages, rows and bytes, checks the endpoint and response scope, and retains original observation clocks. It normalizes kickoff from timestamp, offset and time-zone evidence, maps provider competition, season, team and fixture IDs to canonical keys, and keeps reported goals separate from verified scores. `TBD` and `NS` map to scheduled. Active phases, interruption and suspension map to live. `FT`, `AET` and `PEN` remain distinct played finals. `PST`, `CANC`, `ABD`, `AWD` and `WO` map to postponed, canceled, abandoned or awarded. Complete scoped observations can serve multiple consumers. A partial page, missing fixture or identity conflict is recorded as such. [Provider adapter](next-js/src/server/football/api-football-adapter.ts), [normalization](next-js/src/server/football/api-football-normalize.ts), [catalog service](next-js/src/server/football/catalog-service.ts), [discovery](next-js/src/server/live/live-discovery-import.ts).

**Select and queue.** Daily identity follows midnight in Kampala (21:00 UTC on the preceding UTC date). Eligible accounts can also run hourly selection, skipping the daily boundary. The live plan covers eight Kampala dates to span seven visitor-local dates. Legacy manifests can use seven, and Free plan intake covers two dates. Selection checks allowed competitions, verified scheduled status, known kickoff, the original and applicable rolling windows, unresolved lifecycle issues, cutoff and time to drain the queue. Kickoff order and plan capacity bound the eligible members. The current workload caps planned refresh capacity at 2,000 and clamps queue lead time to 10 minutes–2 hours. The hourly path needs a verified allowance of at least 7,500 requests per day and positive capacity. A reviewed degraded run can seal partial coverage. [Calendar](next-js/src/domain/calendar.ts), [live plan](next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [trigger](next-js/src/server/selection/selection-trigger.ts), [selection store](next-js/src/server/selection/selection-mysql-store.ts), [selection runbook](next-js/docs/daily-selection.md).

**Refresh.** `prediction.daily-refresh` verifies the sealed run member, job, fixture version and cycle. It can prepare context before analysis, then freezes its plan, model pin where applicable, analysis and evidence scope, and resource bounds. The worker seals evidence, resolves the authorized engines, records selection or abstention, and passes a final exact fixture observation through lifecycle checks before publication. A separate status retry is available for a proven zero-dispatch transient admission failure. Old safety evidence can halt writes, while eligibility requires a fresh scheduled observation. Standard preparation can use up to six football requests, followed by one forecast and one status request. Current Local Engine-first jobs use stored inputs and allow one status call plus optional provider fallback. Job time/final reserve is 5 minutes/60 seconds on standard pacing and 8 minutes/200 seconds on slow pacing. [Refresh service](next-js/src/server/refresh/refresh-service.ts), [observation](next-js/src/server/refresh/refresh-observation.ts), [live plan](next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts).

`DailyRunManifest`, `DurableJob`, `PredictionRefreshIntent`, `PredictionRefreshStage` and `PredictionRefreshOutcome` retain membership, attempts and sealed inputs/results. Fenced leases protect ownership. Coordinated work keys on occurrence, schedule, model/market registry and material evidence or lineup identity, coalesces over 60 seconds and limits one active job per fixture under a shared cap. A sealed phase reuses original data after a restart. Shared football quota and separate optional AI/research cost ledgers enforce account/job limits, preserve critical capacity and account for uncertain dispatch. [Job store](next-js/src/server/jobs/job-mysql-store.ts), [refresh store](next-js/src/server/refresh/refresh-mysql-store.ts), [material keys](next-js/src/server/refresh/forecast-orchestration.ts), [quota gateway](next-js/src/server/football/quota-gateway.ts), [cost gateway](next-js/src/server/cost-control/cost-gateway.ts).

## Evidence and point-in-time inputs

A `FixtureEvidenceSnapshot` binds the exact fixture/version, teams, competition and season, kickoff, cutoff, original source clocks and evidence references. Bounds and seals preserve missing, conflicting and unavailable claims. Collection, retention, model transmission and public display have separate authorities. An observation acquired after the cutoff cannot be used to reconstruct an earlier forecast as if available then. [Evidence contract](next-js/src/server/evidence/evidence-contract.ts), [snapshot builder](next-js/src/server/evidence/evidence-snapshot.ts).

| Input | How it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Local goal history | Same canonical competition, verified regulation scores, canonical home/away IDs and result observations available by cutoff. The reader excludes the target, checks canonical status and score against the sealed result and caps the sample at 1,500. Verified history imports can supply results even without a prediction cycle. [History reader](next-js/src/server/intelligence/live-local-history.ts), [model evidence](next-js/src/server/evidence/fixture-model-evidence.ts). |
| Recommendation evidence | Up to eight matches per team in the prior 120 days, with at least three each. Reorient by target team role, preserve actual head-to-head orientation and deduplicate matches. Apply `w = 2^(−ageDays/30)` and event support `(1 + Σ weighted wins)/(2 + Σ weights)` to the exact event. Support below 0.40 vetoes eligibility. An outright team win is also vetoed for three recent non-wins with negative aggregate goal difference. [Baseline evidence](next-js/src/server/evidence/baseline-evidence.ts). |
| Availability and context | An eligible confirmed absence can veto a relevant team win, Over 2.5 or BTTS Yes if the attacker has at least 450 season minutes and three goals plus assists, without a verified starting replacement with comparable contributions and minutes. The rule joins exact player/team/competition/season. Contributions have a seven-day freshness ceiling and lineup/availability reports 24 hours. Missing, doubtful or conflicting reports retain uncertainty. Authorized legacy preparation can fetch histories, injuries, lineups and one player statistics page per team. [Player evidence](next-js/src/server/evidence/player-football.ts), [baseline runbook](next-js/docs/baseline-evidence.md). |

The shared base recommendation audit applies these support rules, including under v3. Local Engine numerical qualification and the expanded pre-match decision use their own model and sample checks. Venue, player, common-opponent and news context can contribute attributable context where bound. The current local fit uses league, teams, time weights and scores. Its probabilities have no fitted injury, tactic or news coefficient. | Path | Resolution |
| --- | --- |

New intents omit remote AI/research. |
| Qualified AI/local/provider contract | `qualified-ai-local-provider-v1` supports independently qualified AI, local, then provider groups. AI requires evaluated nonprovisional output, adequate evidence and proof of a coherent joint score distribution. The packaged legacy AI model does not qualify. | Legacy | Configured `ai-first` (the default for this policy) supports optional provisional AI, then local/provider as configured, with an independent provider baseline. `provider-first` keeps the provider-baseline plan. Frozen work retains its source order, model and receipts. |

A missing or rejected source can fall through within its authorized scope. Match result and derived double chance travel together. Goals and BTTS can survive independently under cross-market checks. Partial new revisions record their own gaps. [Live binding](next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts), [fallback contract](next-js/src/server/fallback/fallback-contract.ts), [resolution](next-js/src/server/fallback/fallback-resolution.ts), [qualified pipeline report](next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-forecast-pipeline-2026-11-20.md).

**Local Engine.** `regularized-poisson-v1:<configHash>` fits independent home and away goal rates at the cutoff. The home log-rate is league baseline + home advantage + home attack + away defense. The away log-rate is baseline + away attack + home defense. The fit minimizes weighted Poisson loss `Σ w × (exp(η) − goals × η)` plus squared regularization. Defaults are a 730-day lookback, `w = 2^(−ageDays/180)`, team ridge 3, 500 iterations, tolerance `1e−7` and supported rates 0.05–6. Weighted support must reach 30 for the league and six for each team. Unseen teams, insufficient evidence, failed convergence, invalid rates or reaching the lock can withhold output. Provenance retains fit parameters, sample and input identities. [Model](next-js/src/server/intelligence/local-goals-model.ts), [fitting](next-js/src/server/intelligence/local-goals-fit.ts), [publication proof](next-js/src/server/intelligence/live-local-engine.ts).

For a rate `λ`, `P(G=k) = exp(−λ) × λᵏ/k!`. 1X2 sums independent score cells by the difference in goals. The base implementation sums through count 60 for home and draw and assigns the remaining mass to away. With `s = λHome + λAway`, `P(Under 2.5) = exp(−s) × (1 + s + s²/2)`. `P(BTTS Yes) = (1−exp(−λHome)) × (1−exp(−λAway))`. Over and BTTS No are complements. [Marginal calculations](next-js/src/server/intelligence/local-goals-model.ts), [count distributions](next-js/src/domain/score-distribution-markets.ts).

**Provider and AI.** Fallback verifies the request, exact fixture/team/league/season identity and clocks before reading the complete `predictions.percent.home/draw/away` group as percentages. Text advice, winner flags and goal strings supply no totals or BTTS probabilities. The live policy caps fixture retrieval age at two days and prediction retrieval age at ten minutes, with known source age capped at two days. Unknown generation can be flagged. An optional AI intent pins the model, prompt, schema, calibration and evidence identity. It sends attributable untrusted evidence without odds or the provider's numerical forecast. The legacy OpenAI binding uses `gpt-6.1-sol` with no calibration. The qualified path supports separate score-grid and calibration contracts. Its coordinated resilience policy permits one separately budgeted transient retry under frozen limits, uses a 60-second dispatch cap and opens a process-local ten-minute circuit after five provider-level failures. [Provider adapter](next-js/src/server/fallback/fallback-adapter.ts), [freshness](next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts), [AI binding](next-js/src/server/live/live-ai-model.ts), [prompt](next-js/src/server/predictor/predictor-prompt.ts), [AI output](next-js/src/server/predictor/predictor-output.ts), [calibration](next-js/src/server/predictor/predictor-calibration.ts), [resilience](next-js/src/server/predictor/predictor-resilience.ts).

## Markets, outcomes and probabilities

The base period is regulation including stoppage time. An exact key can distinguish period, team or other subject, line, outcome and payout rule. An odds catalogue entry establishes an offer identity, while forecast availability depends on the relevant model and evidence. [Base markets](next-js/src/domain/markets.ts), [registry](next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [outcome labels](next-js/docs/prediction-outcomes.md).

| Market | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `match-result` | `1` home win, `X` draw, `2` away win. |
| `double-chance` | `1X` home or draw, `X2` draw or away, `12` home or away. Each sums two events from the same 1X2 group. The three overlap. |
| `total-goals` | Over 2.5 means at least three combined goals. Under 2.5 means zero through two. |
| `both-teams-to-score` | Yes when each side scores at least once. Otherwise No. |

The base groups require all exact keys and finite probabilities strictly between 0 and 1. Exclusive groups must sum to one within 0.001. With `Y = P(BTTS Yes)`, `D = P(draw)` and `O = P(Over 2.5)`, the feasibility checks are `Y ≤ D + O + 0.002` and `D + O ≤ 1 + Y + 0.002`. Conflicts can remove groups under the frozen resolution policy. A family pick is its unrounded maximum, with stable selection order breaking an exact tie. [Validation](next-js/src/domain/markets.ts), [source conflict handling](next-js/src/server/fallback/fallback-resolution.ts).

- **Released Local Engine extensions.** The current `local-regulation-count-v2` profile bounds full-time fixture/home/away totals to lines 1.5 through 5.5 inclusive. Its predecessor applied that bound to fixture totals. Defined half-goal totals have binary outcomes, and eligible integer and quarter lines have Asian payout terms. The profile also includes exact counts 0–6 plus 7+, the ranges 0–1/2–3/4–5/6+, reviewed Yes/No ranges and odd/even. Team counts use the team's rate and combined counts use the sum. [Exact definitions and versioned profiles](next-js/src/domain/goal-market-definitions.ts), [count derivation](next-js/src/domain/score-distribution-markets.ts).
- **Wider score catalogue.** The registry defines period-specific result/double chance, draw-no-bet, Asian and three-way handicap, totals, exact/ranged counts and scores, parity, BTTS, team to score, clean sheet, win to nil, half/full combinations and joint score predicates. Exact score lists home then away, with Other covering the defined complement. Combined selections require each predicate. A handicap applies the stated signed line. Draw-no-bet pushes on a draw. Second-half scores require verified half-time and full-time pairs. A joint period or event market needs support for the joint outcomes. Exact keys and the independently qualified model determine which markets can publish. [Goal catalogue](next-js/src/domain/goal-market-definitions.ts), [market evaluator](next-js/src/server/predictions/forecast-market-evaluator.ts).
- **Other metrics and events.** Reviewed contracts can express totals, result/double chance, handicaps, counts and parity for stored statistics such as cards, corners, booking points, shots, fouls, offsides, saves and other registered counts. Event definitions include any, first or last by team or player, and the first/last event team including None. Period, eligible metrics, counting rule and player appearance/start requirements are exact. The ordinary live plan has no independent numerical model or bound implied extension for these markets. [Non-goal definitions](next-js/src/domain/non-goal-market-definitions.ts), [capabilities](next-js/src/server/intelligence/market-evidence-capabilities.ts), [live plan](next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts).

Extended distributions preserve outright win, loss, push, half win, half loss and unrepresented mass. A quarter line splits into its neighboring half-step lines, so a win plus a push gives half win and a loss plus a push gives half loss. `winProbability` is outright win, with upper bound `min(1, win + residual)`. A grid can withhold a selection if the residual could change the leader. The reviewed grid extension requires residual below `0.000001`. Confidence orders the strength of support (`calibrated`, `supported`, `limited`) separately from estimated outcome probability. [Payout rules](next-js/src/domain/market-payout.ts), [score-grid contract](next-js/src/domain/score-distribution.ts), [decision receipt](next-js/src/server/predictions/forecast-decision.ts).

## Odds, ranking and featured picks

Odds are stored observations of offers. Pre-match, closing, historical and in-play reads carry separate source clocks and rules. Three-hour pre-match sweeps and bounded page recovery use spare quota and exact fixture/bookmaker/bet/outcome identity. A suspended, stale or mismatched offer stays unavailable. Decimal price `d` has raw implied weight `1/d`. The separate authorized implied path uses proportional normalization `coverage × (1/d)/Σ(1/d)` within a complete single-book group, with coverage 1 for partitions and 2 for mapped double chance. Asian implied values use a conditional payout-price basis. Independent provider, Local Engine and AI estimates retain their own probabilities. Collection/retention, internal ranking and public odds display require their applicable rights. [Odds service](next-js/src/server/odds/odds-service.ts), [current and historical odds runbook](next-js/docs/provider-odds.md), [implied market derivation](next-js/src/domain/market-implied-markets.ts), [display gate](next-js/src/server/odds/public-odds-policy.ts).

| Sealed decision | Ranking and guards |
| --- | --- |
| `probability-qualified-odds-v1` / `probability-then-odds-v2` | Within each eligible source pool, v1 sorts by price then probability, and v2 by probability then price. Stable family/selection order resolves the final tie. Both require at least 65%, legacy football evidence and an exact permitted price. Source preference is eligible AI, local, then retained provider. |
| `probability-confidence-odds-v3` | The shared audit uses an inclusive zero floor and retains the baseline evidence assessment. The order is unrounded probability, confidence, eligible exact price if present, then stable market/outcome identity. |
| `prematch-forecast-decision-v2` | The expanded Local Engine-first decision seals exact distributions, sources, confidence, support and unavailability. It ranks positive outright-win per-market maxima including tied alternatives, consults prices only at probability/confidence ties, and allows an unpriced pick. |

The shared recommendation has a 24-hour forecast retrieval ceiling and a three-hour quote ceiling, with quote observation by analysis cutoff and stricter source policies where applicable. A recommendation can abstain while valid families still publish. Its archived decision stays tied to the original prices. The featured policy below is a separate default-display choice and checks quotes against its own 12-hour freshness bound. ### Local Engine featured policy

The released `local-featured-pre-match-v3` takes all supported selections in accepted Local Engine base groups and eligible goal extensions. It avoids counting the extended full-time 2.5 total twice when the legacy group is available. It requires an exact fixture occurrence and a pre-kickoff decision clock. The live display decision runs before normal publication deadline. At cutoff, a distinct operation can freeze the accepted revision's decision using effective close minus one millisecond. A revision must already exist at that clock. A corrected start at or before the decision can suppress the frozen display. [Candidate collection](next-js/src/server/matches/upcoming-featured-pick.ts), [cutoff decision](next-js/src/server/predictions/cutoff-featured.ts), [frozen read](next-js/src/server/matches/frozen-featured-picks.ts).

| Tier, in precedence order | Eligibility and ordering                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Standard                  | Verified exact price at least `1.10`, strictly positive modeled net return and an outright win probability of at least 60%. Use the highest populated rung in `95%, 90%, 85%, 80%, 75%, 70%, 65%, 60%`, then higher exact odds, higher unrounded probability and stable market/selection identity. |
| Priced fallback           | A remaining verified exact price at least `1.01` and the same probability ladder. Prefer positive return first, then higher rung, odds, probability and stable identity. Break-even and negative return are retained as such.                                                                        |
| Forecast-only             | A reviewed, fully represented estimate with outright win probability at least 60%. Prefer demonstrated comparative coverage where supplied, then reviewed common markets and central lines where coverage is absent, then probability and stable identity.                                             |
| Abstention                | An invalid clock, absence of modeled outcomes or lack of a qualifying probability can leave the featured selection empty. All Markets can still retain valid forecasts.                                                                                                                                |

The best exact offer for a selection uses the highest stored decimal price, with lower bookmaker ID breaking a price tie. The binding checks bet ID, bet name and exact selection text. Featured pricing supports the four legacy bindings and mapped binary half-goal match and team totals, including mapped half-time match totals when an eligible model exists. Integer and quarter lines need a separately verified payout binding. The source requires a complete represented grade distribution, coherent pre-match observation clocks, matching kickoff and fresh observed and provider-update times when present. The older `v1` has a 75% last rung, and `v2` goes down to 60%. Both older policies stop at standard priced eligibility. [Featured policy and bindings](next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [odds mapping](next-js/src/domain/odds-markets.ts), [reference freshness](next-js/src/domain/reference-odds.ts).

For a unit stake and decimal odds `d`, modeled net return is `pWin × (d − 1) − pLoss + pHalfWin × (d − 1)/2 − pHalfLoss/2`. Push contributes zero. For a complete binary event it is `pWin × d − 1`. The featured code performs the signed comparison with serialized probabilities and scaled integer odds. The display-only `V` marker requires estimated net return of at least 5%. The probability, grade distribution, odds and modeled return are separate measures. [Value and ranking implementation](next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [featured presentation](next-js/src/server/matches/featured-pick-presentation.ts).

**Worked example with illustrative inputs.** Suppose a qualifying Local Engine revision gives `1 = 0.52`, `X = 0.28`, `2 = 0.20` for regulation time. Derived `1X = 0.80`, `X2 = 0.48` and `12 = 0.72`. Suppose only `1X` has a permitted, exact, timely quote, at `1.30`. It qualifies on the 80% standard rung because `0.80 × 1.30 − 1 = 0.04`. Its quoted break-even probability is about 76.92%, and the 4% modeled return is below the `V` threshold. If the same pick is frozen at close and the verified regulation score is `0–0`, settlement marks `1X` Correct. This example follows selection and settlement rules using invented probabilities, price and score. [Market validation](next-js/src/domain/markets.ts), [featured decision](next-js/src/domain/local-featured-decision.ts), [settlement rules](next-js/src/domain/market-settlement.ts).

## Publication, locking and settlement

Publication preserves what was known before play. Result processing grades that locked forecast against verified evidence of what happened.

### Publication and cutoff

Publication verifies the manifest, sealed inputs, source/quote authority, fixture version, schedule, status, cycle, lease and database clock under the shared provider-then-fixture lock. Before the deadline it requires matching scheduled status, no unresolved lifecycle issue or early start, and a final status observation at most two minutes old. Live source freshness separately caps AI retrieval at six hours, Local Engine generation at one hour and API-Football retrieval at one hour. Publication can tolerate unknown remote clocks only under their explicit policy. [Publication transaction](next-js/src/server/predictions/publication-service.ts), [eligibility](next-js/src/server/predictions/publication-eligibility.ts), [freshness policy](next-js/src/server/live/live-plan.ts).

Acceptance appends an immutable `PredictionSet`, four `MarketPrediction` rows including explicit unavailable groups, any approved extensions/quotes, refresh result and change event. It moves `PredictionCycle.currentSetId` atomically. At most one set is accepted per run/fixture/cycle, and an outdated run cannot replace newer work. If all groups fail, the worker can retain an eligible past revision at its original clocks or record unavailability. A retry of the same sealed outcome returns the original. `GoalMarketExtension` and conditional `ImpliedMarketExtension` share exact revision/cycle binding and have compact published-pick projections. [History store](next-js/src/server/predictions/history-mysql-store.ts), [revision publication](next-js/docs/revision-publication.md), [extension persistence](next-js/docs/extended-market-persistence.md), [schema](next-js/prisma/schema.prisma).

Normal write cutoff is kickoff minus five minutes. Cutoff reconciliation preserves an already-reached deadline under the schedule then in force and respects proven earlier play. It chooses the latest eligible revision actually published strictly before effective close and seals `lockedSetId`; a cycle can close without a lock. A postponement voids its previous cycle and a verified reschedule can open a new one. Later schedule, team or early-start evidence can invalidate the original lock. [Cutoff](next-js/src/server/predictions/cutoff-service.ts), [schedule-time eligibility](next-js/src/server/predictions/cutoff-eligibility.ts), [lifecycle](next-js/src/server/predictions/lifecycle-service.ts).

### Result and grade

The result worker uses scoped live/date observations and batches of up to 20 exact IDs. It validates, seals and applies result observations with their original clocks. Disappearance from a live list cannot establish a final. `FixtureResultState` points to the current verified `FixtureResult`. Played finals use independently verified `score.fulltime` for regulation grading. Extra-time and shootout totals stay separate, and missing phase evidence can continue to trigger rechecks. Period markets require their own verified score proof. The ordinary live adapter supplies the regulation verifier, while verified half-time needs a separate binding. [Result sync](next-js/src/server/results/result-sync-service.ts), [result writer](next-js/src/server/results/result-write.ts), [phase rules](next-js/src/server/results/result-phases.ts), [live runtime](next-js/src/server/live/live-runtime.ts).

| State | Outcome for the applicable selection |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduled, in play, unclear status, or played final lacking required proof | Pending for an available locked pick. Missing facts never mean zero. |
| Coherent verified final | Grade the exact locked selection as Correct or Incorrect, with Push, Half win or Half loss where the exact extended payout supports them. |
| Postponed, canceled, abandoned, awarded, or invalidated cycle/lock | Void for an attributable locked pick. Preserve the historical reason and occurrence. |
| Missing or ineligible lock or family | Unavailable. |
| Corrected evidence | Append a predecessor-linked settlement revision, even when the grade is unchanged if its result version changed. While correction proof is unresolved, stale grades lose counting eligibility. |

An implied statistic requires the matching verified metric, period and counting rule. Event markets require a verified complete ordered sequence, ignore overturned events and wait on conflicting evidence. Player nonappearance or failure to start can void when required and verified. `SettlementBatch`, `MarketSettlementRevision`, extended equivalents and per-event receipts record atomic change and suppress identical repeats. Reconciliation repairs missing projections without rerunning the forecast. Canonical binary performance counts only current Correct/Incorrect picks on applicable closed cycles. ## Recalculation, replay and private models

The ordinary worker refreshes eligible pre-match work. Backfill, retrospective display and private evaluation use separate paths and evidence clocks.

### Recover or refresh

Hourly forecast recovery reconciles sealed attempts and missing outcomes within the existing deadline. Valid new material can create a new eligible revision, while price-only changes do not change the coordinated numerical job identity. The upcoming featured policy can use changing prices until its own decision freezes. `history:backfill` processes bounded source pages, coverage, details and reference data or replays retained observations. It can supply inputs or repair results under original availability clocks. Historical recovery cannot establish an original forecast or quote that was never captured. [Forecast recovery](next-js/src/server/refresh/forecast-recovery.ts), [history CLI](next-js/scripts/backfill-football-history.mjs), [backfill service](next-js/src/server/football/history-backfill.ts), [watchdog](next-js/docs/recovery-watchdog.md).

### Retrospective projection

`npm run november:replay` audits a Kampala date range, creates a resumable dry-run checkpoint and stages a separately authorized projection. Its default window is November 1 inclusive to December 1 exclusive in Kampala. Explicit `--from` and `--to` take inclusive Kampala dates. The cohort includes displaced occurrences for audit, and still-open fixtures remain prospective. Dry run prefers eligible original sealed Local Engine distributions. A rebuild needs a proven pre-match schedule and pre-cutoff result availability. It can withhold an estimate when the required evidence clocks or identities are absent. The price clock is effective lock minus one millisecond, separate from the model's evidence cutoff. Only exact, attributable point-in-time offers qualify. The evaluator joins verified outcomes after decisions are fixed. [Replay CLI](next-js/scripts/november-replay.mjs), [evidence resolution](next-js/src/server/intelligence/november-replay-evidence.ts), [market reconstruction](next-js/src/server/intelligence/november-replay-model.ts), [featured replay](next-js/src/server/intelligence/november-replay-featured.ts).

`NovemberReplayRevision`, `NovemberReplayProjection` and `NovemberReplayAction` retain staged estimates, the separate active pointer and activation history. The projection read checks fixture, cycle, kickoff and source revision against the occurrence. It exposes retrospective provenance and can preserve a sealed abstention. Activation and rollback affect that projection while preserving the original publications, locks, prices and settlement ledger. Quote coverage diagnostics distinguish an eligible exact offer from an unbound or unqualified market. The three-year history inventory measures retained evidence coverage. The forecast rebuild uses the original model's configured training window. The private historical ranking experiment uses earlier resolved originals for comparable outcome, probability and context, with minimum effective samples of 40, 50 or 80 across its backoff pools.
