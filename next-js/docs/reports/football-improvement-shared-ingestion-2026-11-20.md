## Runtime and deployment

The private response switch `GOAL_HINT_FOOTBALL_RESPONSE_ARCHIVE_ENABLED` defaults to `false`. It requires existing private retention authority and the production worker or a qualified isolated target. The archive reuses the existing worker's `FootballHistorySourcePage` read and insert grants. Scoped checkpoint writers also need `SELECT`, `INSERT` and `UPDATE` on the coverage revision, status, reason, attempt and timestamp columns. Current staging and production provider settings were read during the audit. This step did not enable new provider calls or spend provider quota.

The worker retains the direct subscription channel and shared durable quota account. Verified short-window limits use an 80% allowance, with the owner's tighter limits applied where configured. Unknown short-window limits use five requests per minute and one concurrent dispatch. Historical work has the lowest priority, a 20% per-minute cap, and reserves 120% of the estimated remaining critical requests plus 5% of the daily limit. Account timing follows the direct provider day. It retains the existing 60-second boundary guard because provider rate windows can span midnight. Marketplace accounts follow a different reset schedule and have no authorized adapter in this checkout.
