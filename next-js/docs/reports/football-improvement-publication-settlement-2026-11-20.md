## Reporting denominators

The Matches summary counts each fixture once in its date and filter cohort. Its shown-pick breakdown separates won, lost, pending, void, cancelled and unavailable. The forecast audit scores each market family separately for the applicable locked revision. Its resolved-pick hit rate is `correct / (correct + incorrect)`. Pending, void, cancelled, unavailable and alternative outcomes do not enter that denominator. Fixture counts and resolved market samples can differ when one fixture contributes several supported market families.

The admin read model records the discovered count and its receipt completeness separately from stored, public-scope, listed, forecast-published, prediction-ready, priced and awaiting fixtures. A bounded private read enumerates actual excluded fixture IDs and their reasons. It also separates market-prediction counts from fixture totals, partial or unavailable sources from complete checkpoints, and pre-match catalog mapping from live mapping. Worker diagnostics use durable queue and lease state, job outcomes and provider-day quota and reset records. Missing telemetry remains unavailable.
