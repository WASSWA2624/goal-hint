## Reported symptoms and current source behavior

The five 14 November reference screenshots were recovered from Git commit `ef8450bf`. At 20:50 EAT the admin capture shows 1,008 stored fixtures, all 1,008 in public scope, and 674 published. The 20:34 mobile view shows 674 rows and several API-sourced double-chance picks displayed at 90%. The admin capture also shows a failed fixture receipt, no recent odds catalogs or prices, historical phases waiting on quota, and unavailable odds mapping. These are point-in-time observations.

`readFootballCoverage` in `server/admin/football-coverage.ts` counts `published` when `displayedSet` is present. The public `feedSql` independently requires an available published market for current and future rows. Its historical exception includes canonical terminal fixtures without forecasts in an unfiltered archive. The broad `publicFixtureScope` check and the forecast-dependent feed filter explain how the admin and public row populations can diverge. The fixture-level evidence below distinguishes the retained now-current archive from the original screenshot.

The failed-receipt line reports the latest daily attempt's failure flag in the absence of a usable receipt. The odds lines count batches from the last 24 hours. The history state is persisted per phase, and `history-import-jobs.ts` maps quota denial, rate limit and provider day end to waiting on quota. The odds mapping path is gated by runtime odds policy and verified permissions. A label by itself cannot distinguish transport, coverage, quota, policy or rights failures. `readFootballCoverage` also catches query failures independently, which can produce an unknown count.

The kickoff timestamp and the observed provider phase are separate. `result-sync-policy.ts` tracks results around kickoff and schedules unresolved retries. `selection-fixture-reconciliation.ts` supplies an hourly provider recheck and scoped lookup, with the same quota and durable work infrastructure. Feed snapshots and clocks reflect accepted provider observations. Scheduled kickoff crossing does not itself establish a live match or score. Diagnosis of individual overdue fixtures needs their result receipts and tracked poll state.

`normalizeFallbackPrediction` reads API-Football's home/draw/away percentages. `fallback-adapter.ts` converts those complete values to fractions. `domain/markets.ts` derives double chance by summing the two mutually exclusive match-result probabilities and applies display rounding later. In the public read the highest available family probability is selected across markets when public prices are disabled. Double chance therefore has a structural advantage under raw probability ranking. The live provider path has no fixed 90% default. In the local 14 November snapshot, the first 100 returned forecasts all use provider double chance. Ninety-four have an exact maximum of 90% and six have 70%. One revision with 45% home, 45% draw and 10% away yields 90% for 1X. Another with 10%, 45% and 45% yields 90% for X2. This verifies the mechanism for those stored revisions. The screenshots and local snapshot show separate observations.
