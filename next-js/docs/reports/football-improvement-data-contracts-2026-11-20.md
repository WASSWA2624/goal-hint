- `server/football/api-football-inventory.ts` records the API-Football paths and selector variants, required scope, paging and batching, refresh cadence, historical limits, and field disposition. [API-Football v3 documentation](https://www.api-football.com/documentation-v3)
- `server/football/provider-coverage-contract.ts` validates a canonical resource, scope, endpoint, page and section. Distinct provider league, season, team, fixture, player, coach, venue and bookmaker identities can coexist in a scope. Coverage records distinguish all ten requested states, record the last response and completion separately, keep failure and retry information, and preserve an unknown remaining count. A structurally successful empty page has a response receipt. Completion requires separate evidence from the endpoint and page rules.
- `FootballProviderCoverage` persists the checkpoint and refers to its last successful response in the existing private `FootballHistorySourcePage` archive. The `raw:` archive namespace isolates full envelopes from historical row projections. `prepareProviderResponsePage` keeps original bytes, explicit nulls and unknown fields, checks the endpoint and page, and records a SHA-256 digest. It takes response bytes and validated query parameters. HTTP headers and credentials belong to the transport. Account status remains private.
- `domain/market-registry.ts` declares 30 versioned market entries. It refers to the existing goal and non-goal rule versions and separates provider forecast, pre-match odds, and live odds namespaces. Each entry describes periods, selections, parameter dimensions, evidence and model capability. A registry entry does not establish that a bookmaker offers it for a fixture or that a prediction model can evaluate it. Player, statistic and cross-period gaps have explicit unsupported states. The source supplied a public market vocabulary reference. Fixture-specific prices and the complete market list remain subject to qualified catalog evidence.
