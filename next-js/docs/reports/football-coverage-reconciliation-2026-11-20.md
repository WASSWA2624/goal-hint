Its 333 missing revisions consisted of 317 closed cycles with no locked revision, eight fixtures with no cycle, and eight void cycles with no retained revision. Its staging 20 November cohort contains 334 fixtures without a cycle. Those are different identified populations. ## Operational denominators

| Field | Population |
| --- | --- |
| Discovered | Rows observed in the latest retained exact-date fixture receipt known by the report time. The receipt exposes its own observed time, completeness, rejection count and any recorded missing provider IDs. Missing or failed receipts leave discovery unknown. |
| Stored | Distinct API-Football fixtures with a canonical filing or kickoff on the EAT reporting date, plus canonical fixture IDs retained in that date's latest receipt. The latter captures an observed fixture that subsequently moves or loses its kickoff. |
| Public scope and published | Distinct stored fixtures with a kickoff in the EAT interval and the same competition and retained-cycle predicate as the ordinary Matches list. Forecast, price and section availability are independent of fixture listing. |
| Excluded | Stored fixtures outside that listing population. A private paginated read provides the exact canonical and provider IDs with missing kickoff, kickoff outside the reporting date, or competition outside public scope. |
| Forecast published and prediction ready | Displayed immutable revision present, and displayed revision with an available supported legacy family, respectively. A second private paginated read lists in-scope fixtures without a usable forecast and their cycle or market reason. |
| Market predictions | Available legacy family rows plus modeled extended market picks on the displayed revisions of in-scope fixtures. Implied bookmaker estimates are outside the modeled prediction tally. |
| Priced | Fixtures in scope with at least one eligible stored bookmaker projection and an applicable latest odds receipt. The observation and known provider clock must be within the freshness window, evaluated at kickoff for valid frozen closing prices. Rights or mapping limitations can leave an observed odds fixture without a fresh projected price. |
| Awaiting data | In-scope fixtures at or past kickoff with source status still scheduled or unknown. The audit includes the original provider code, retrieval time, next result check and any stored delay reason. |

The coverage report counts fixtures and market predictions separately. Binary hit rates in the performance reports use resolved wins and losses. Pending or unavailable predictions, cancellations, voids, pushes and partial Asian grades stay outside that binary denominator. Corrections use the authoritative current settlement revision.

The owner-backed reader reports archive checkpoints and the stored provider coverage statuses touched in the previous 24 hours. Mapping counts use separately observed pre-match and in-play catalogs. Queue lag is the age of the oldest eligible ready job. Worker last success and error timestamps use the prior 24 hours. Result polling ownership comes from active leases, and last completed receipt time measures success independently of a lease. A unique active result poller identifies the provider-day quota scope. When ownership is ambiguous the admin quota reading is unavailable. The live runner emits a separate process heartbeat with its last quota observation, next scheduled times and current estimated reserve. These reads and events require no provider dispatch.
