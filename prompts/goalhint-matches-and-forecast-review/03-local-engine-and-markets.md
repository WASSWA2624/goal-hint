# 03 — Forecast supported markets with the Local Engine first

Use the [shared registry](../../next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts), [per-market evaluator](../../next-js/src/server/predictions/forecast-market-evaluator.ts) and [forecast orchestration](../../next-js/src/server/refresh/forecast-orchestration.ts). Apply [§6.6, market evaluation](../goalhint-improvement-prompt.md#66-shared-market-registry-and-per-market-evaluation) and [§6.7, stored output and ranking](../goalhint-improvement-prompt.md#67-top-pick-stored-output-and-historical-integrity). The [model report](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-evidence-models-2026-11-20.md) leaves fitted and period models diagnostic. The later [hosted follow-up](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-rollout-2026-11-20.md#backfill-odds-and-local-engine-follow-up) records qualified baseline Local Engine publications.

## Implementation

- Version new decisions so each market instance tries the qualified Local Engine first. Admit an existing trusted API-Football prediction only where the Local Engine genuinely cannot produce a supported result and the fallback independently qualifies. Provide a way to disable fallback as local coverage improves. Preserve source attribution and replay semantics for older sealed decisions and intents.
- Reconcile the registry against the available provider catalogs. Evaluate each applicable, legitimately registered fixture/period/line/subject instance. Include results and double chance, draw-no-bet, Asian and European handicaps, match and team goals, clean sheets, BTTS, correct scores, exact counts and ranges, half relationships, combinations, player props, cards, corners and other qualified offerings. Full-match Over/Under must include every half-goal line from 0.5 through 6.5 and other legitimate registered lines. Keep outright wins, losses, pushes and half grades distinct for integer and quarter lines.
- Use coherent score distributions, qualified joint and period models for dependent events, and suitable participation and statistical evidence for player, cards and corner markets. Evaluate qualification on chronological holdouts; abstain with explicit unsupported or insufficient-evidence reasons when the model or evidence falls short. Store each supported outcome distribution and recommended outcome with version, cutoff, provenance and confidence even when no bookmaker price is available. Keep odds independently joined to the exact selection.
- Reuse [recommendation](../../next-js/src/domain/recommendation.ts) and [market ranking](../../next-js/src/domain/market-ranking.ts) to place eligible outcomes by descending probability, confidence, comparable decimal odds and stable market/selection key. Eligibility depends on evidence. Rankings need no diversity quota.

## Acceptance

Verify exact and monotonic totals through 6.5, overlapping double chance, joint probability bounds, quarter-line settlement, local-first routing, fallback disablement, explicit abstention and publication without prices. Compare calibration, proper scores and coverage on chronological holdouts before enabling each model scope. Update [APP_OVERVIEW.md](../../APP_OVERVIEW.md) with implemented eligibility, model limits and actual activation status.
