Use the implemented components and theme tokens as the baseline, with the older PNGs in `prompts/mobile-ui-updated-Package/` as visual inspiration only. ## Implementation

- **Date navigation.** In `next-js/src/components/match/feed-dates.tsx`, `DayStrip` scrolls by `visible - 1` cells. Make Next expose one additional day and Previous expose one earlier day per click. Keep arrow browsing independent of selection, including when more days are appended or prepended. Preserve swipe, keyboard focus and reduced motion. Tapping a day applies that single day. Calendar edits stay in draft until applied, and Cancel or Escape preserves the applied view. Retain date and range validation, the browsing timezone and existing URL behavior.
- **Pull-to-refresh.** Reproduce the transient or doubled border and identify its owner during the gesture, loading and completion in both themes. Inspect `next-js/src/app/_components/feed-surface.tsx`, `use-feed-pagination.ts`, `use-live-refresh.ts`, and the Flutter host’s `flutter/lib/web/web_view_pull_to_refresh.dart` and `web_app_shell.dart`. The host currently uses a document reload and native indicators. Route a Matches pull into the existing in-page read flow as a foreground refresh, with Flutter providing only gesture transport where needed. Use the shared `NavigationProgress` immediately below the app header and mark the updating region `aria-busy`. Keep current controls, rows, metrics, scroll position and focus stable. Coalesce overlapping requests, retain content on failure and keep passive polling and lazy media quiet. Remove competing feedback on this path after confirming its source.
- **Performance.** Use the full cohort defined by applied filters and accepted search before pagination or outcome drilldown. `next-js/src/domain/feed-performance-summary.ts` already removes the result drilldown, and `next-js/src/server/matches/feed-summary-service.ts` provides the full total, while `next-js/src/components/performance/performance-summary.tsx` currently renders `predictedPerformanceSummary(counts).total` for the Matches All card. Display the full total in All. Count Won, Lost, Pending, Void and Canceled using the same forecast-outcome classification and revision used by the drilldown in `next-js/src/server/matches/feed-result-filter.ts`. Keep unavailable forecasts distinct from Pending, retain forecast-availability counts and make the denominator for outcome percentages clear. An outcome click narrows the list and returns it to page 1. Keep metrics steady until the base cohort, viewer or underlying data changes. Preserve the shared component’s behavior on other screens.
- **Match cards.** In `next-js/src/components/match/match-card.tsx`, `match-status.tsx` and `score-presentation.tsx`, remove the reported live-status clock glyph and omit the visible “Live” prefix when elapsed play is available. Reuse the observed clock in `next-js/src/domain/match-clock.ts` for elapsed time, stoppage and paused phases. Show compact localized statuses with accessible full text. Show verified post-extra-time scores with AET and each team’s verified shootout total beside its own score with PEN, as in home `1 (3)`, away `1 (4)`. The current feed snapshot exposes regulation and live scores. Project verified phase scores through `next-js/src/server/matches/fixture-read.ts` and `next-js/src/domain/fixture-snapshot.ts` as needed, with compatible fallbacks for older or incomplete records. Keep regulation, extra time and shootouts separate in the data and settlement rules. Show a supported local pick probability alongside the existing missing-price marker when odds are unavailable. Preserve concise unavailable and access-locked states and keep model, recalculation and odds provenance details off the cards.
