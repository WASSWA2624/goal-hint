# 02 — Recover and display exact bookmaker odds

Goal Hint's owner confirms it has full rights to publish odds returned by its API for in-app analysis. Use that recorded decision through the existing odds settings and viewer access rules. Collection scope is all obtainable mapped prices across bookmakers, markets and lines. Review the [odds coverage report](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-odds-coverage-2026-11-20.md) and [hosted follow-up](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-rollout-2026-11-20.md#backfill-odds-and-local-engine-follow-up).

## Implementation

- Extend the [pre-match importer](../../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-service.ts), whose current-offer path skips started fixtures, with a distinct historical pre-match recovery path for live and completed fixtures. Recover all obtainable pre-match offers, keep upcoming collection running, and prioritize quotes about to expire from the provider window. Use saved observations wherever available. API-Football documents seven days of pre-match history with variable coverage and no in-play archive. Older pre-match offers and past in-play offers depend on prior capture. [API-Football documentation](https://www.api-football.com/documentation-v3)
- Keep historical and current pre-match reads separate from the [in-play collector](../../next-js/src/server/odds/in-play-odds-service.ts). Capture mapped in-play offers during play within the live quota. Preserve separate catalog IDs, fixture occurrence and kickoff, exact market, period, line, selection, bookmaker or known source, and price. Preserve the reported provider quote/update clock and actual retrieval clock. Unknown quote times stay unknown, and post-kickoff retrieval must never establish that Goal Hint had the price before kickoff.
- Reuse atomic page checkpoints, idempotent structured snapshots and shared quota admission. Reconcile newly mapped exact offers from stored observations where possible, record unavailable or unmapped scope and avoid withdrawals based on partial coverage. Keep the [existing closing-price reader](../../next-js/src/server/odds/odds-mysql-store.ts), frozen publication prices and forecast identity intact. Recovered prices retain historical provenance even when joined for display. This scope uses existing forecasts and collects prices independently.
- Expose exact eligible prices through the existing price presentation and access rules. Keep the compact odds field and show the supported probability when its price is missing. Use the existing unavailable state where needed. [Mobile Matches mockup](mobile-matches.png)
