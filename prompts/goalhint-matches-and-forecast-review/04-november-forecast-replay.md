# 04 — Replay and safely activate November forecasts

Audit the entire November 2026 Matches browsing cohort before changing data. Anchor the operational manifest to November 1 through December 1 exclusive in `Africa/Kampala`, and verify date boundaries against the Matches browsing-zone behavior. Use [chronological forecast evaluation](../../next-js/docs/forecast-evaluation.md), [private Local Engine capture](../../next-js/src/server/intelligence/local-forecast-shadow.ts), [cutoff eligibility](../../next-js/src/server/predictions/cutoff-eligibility.ts) and the [existing publication service](../../next-js/src/server/predictions/publication-service.ts). Review the [model evaluation](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-evidence-models-2026-11-20.md) and [hosted follow-up](../../next-js/docs/reports/football-improvement-rollout-2026-11-20.md#backfill-odds-and-local-engine-follow-up) before claiming any model qualifies.

## Implementation

- Start with a read-only audit by fixture occurrence, league and team. Inventory published forecast source and completeness, trusted fallbacks, unforecast fixtures, verified schedules and cutoff clocks, and evidence known by cutoff. Capture evidence gaps and reproducible cohort and model identities before replay. The full-month forecast audit is independent of the provider's shorter recoverable odds window.
- Rerun the qualified Local Engine across the month. Send fixtures still before their lock through normal prospective processing and continue admitting eligible November fixtures as the month progresses. For started or played fixtures, reconstruct as-of inputs and model/calibration provenance from verified pre-cutoff evidence. Exclude later results, corrections, post-cutoff captures and any training leakage from features. Use authoritative post-match evidence only for evaluation. Record abstention when the evidence cannot support a forecast.
- Stage separately labeled retrospective revisions with actual generation and evidence-cutoff clocks. Where a verified local revision qualifies, let the revised-history display supersede the API-sourced pick using a reversible projection shared by Matches, exact-outcome filters and Match Details. Retain original immutable picks, prices, outcomes and published performance as audit evidence. Keep retrospective evaluation separate from original pre-match results. Preserve forecaster source distinctions in data and keep the existing compact presentation. A nonqualifying replay leaves any eligible original fallback available with its original provenance.
- Checkpoint per occurrence with the evidence hash and engine/registry versions. Provide a resumable dry run, a reviewable activation diff, an idempotent activation and rollback to the original projection. Produce a short internal report with replaced, retained fallback, abstained or insufficient-evidence, and overall coverage totals. Compare local and prior forecasts on comparable chronological holdouts, with sample sizes, accuracy, calibration and coverage.

## Acceptance

Verify month boundaries, replay idempotence, safe activation and rollback, stale or changed schedules, and that later evidence changes cannot affect earlier eligible features. Verify historical pre-match performance remains reproducible from original publications and replay timestamps state when revisions were generated. Update [APP_OVERVIEW.md](../../APP_OVERVIEW.md) with replay behavior, coverage limits and actual activation status.
