## Filters

- Build on `next-js/src/components/match/feed-filters.tsx`, `next-js/src/components/ui/advanced-filter-dialog.tsx` and `next-js/src/domain/feed-filter-draft.ts`. Preserve match status, sort, Probability and Odds bounds, Picks Only, Markets and useful guidance and validation. Reset edits the draft, Apply commits and resets pagination as appropriate, and Close or Escape discards edits. Preserve the applied count and search behavior, access-controlled fields and focus restoration.
- In **Date and time window**, reuse the Matches date-strip and calendar pattern from `next-js/src/components/match/feed-dates.tsx` and `date-calendar.tsx` with a draft-only selection mode. Keep the start and end times, date range and single-day behavior, validation, the browsing timezone and the Live status window rules. Date navigation and child calendar edits must leave the applied feed unchanged until Filters is applied.
- Put **Countries** and **Leagues** in one aligned row. Reuse `SearchableSelect` and its anchored, bounded option list, with clear search, checked states and readable labels, including long entries. Keep selection limits, logos when available, keyboard and touch support, and empty and selected states. Check narrow widths with the keyboard open and preserve the shared action and field heights.

## Markets

- Use the existing `MarketFilterDialog` and a distinct, scrollable picker with a search field and collapsible groups. Group every supported published offering selectable at the viewer’s current access level by family and, where applicable, period, parameter or team, line and outcome. Keep selections visible or summarized while searching and collapsing groups. Reuse localized labels and support names, standard shorthands and exact lines. Include full-match Over and Under at each half-goal line from 0.5 through 6.5, alongside other applicable registered offerings. Keep unsupported and unpublished options out of exact-outcome choices. The shared registry defines eligibility and identity. - Start with `next-js/src/domain/market-registry.ts`, `feed-market-picks.ts`, `market-filter-search.ts`, `feed-query.ts` and `next-js/src/components/match/feed-market-filters.tsx`. Replace the Matches picker’s fixed exact-outcome list, which has a special case for 1.5, with registry-backed canonical identities. Preserve broad family versus precise outcome intent, existing selections and labels, persisted URL compatibility, and other callers of the shared picker. Child Apply updates only the parent draft. Child Cancel or Escape returns to Filters without changing the parent’s prior market choices.
- In `next-js/src/server/matches/feed-read.ts`, extend exact-outcome filtering to use the canonical identity and the revision visible to the viewer before counting, summarizing and paging. Match exact period, line, parameters and selected outcome. Keep the ranking and displayed pick aligned with the matched revision and existing access rules. Preserve valid empty results and prevent search and summaries from exposing gated outcomes.
