## Implementation

- Keep polling live fixtures for source-reported phase, elapsed and added time, and scores. Recover missing results and scores for every provider-covered fixture stuck on Pending, across current dates and the full accessible archive. Use authoritative fixture lookups for absent, stale or contradictory status, result or score evidence, and fetch supported missing phase details. Preserve last verified observations, source status and next recheck across midnight. Distinguish fixtures genuinely awaiting play or settlement from overdue fixtures whose source data is unresolved. A passed kickoff or missing live row can enter awaiting-update state. Final status and settlement require verified evidence.
- Keep regulation, extra-time and shootout scores distinct. Apply newer authoritative corrections idempotently to the canonical database fixture and result rows, and reconcile affected forecast settlements through the existing service. Preserve verified regulation evidence for rules that settle at regulation and keep fixtures on the unresolved recheck path when any required evidence is missing. Ensure refreshed Matches reads expose the corrected statuses and scores.
- Reuse the hourly scheduler, request sharing, leases, coverage receipts and shared quota. Discover today and sweep every provider-covered upcoming day within actual capacity, resuming deferred scope without treating today's success as complete forward coverage. Prefer verified narrow queries and existing fresh receipts. Checkpoint each completed scope, prioritize changed, stale and missing rows, and update canonical records only when data changes. If the provider requires a broad read, keep reconciliation incremental.
- Use remaining capacity for a resumable repair pass from yesterday backward. Reuse dated and competition/season checkpoints and repair missing fixtures, results and supported details. Protect live work ahead of repair. Report dates and fixtures scanned, verified result and score recoveries, settlements corrected, genuine pending states, and unresolved source gaps with their reasons. Track complete, partial, unavailable, deferred and failed scopes separately. Check the documented catalog coordination failures when validating recovery.
