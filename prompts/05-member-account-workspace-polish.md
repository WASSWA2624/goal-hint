# Polish the signed-in member Account workspace

Use [02-member-account-layout.md](02-member-account-layout.md) as the baseline. Refine the existing Account sections and responsive workspace without changing membership rules or unrelated screens. Use the supplied observations as interface references. Obtain approval before a broader redesign of an established interface.

## Current source and starting points

- `next-js/src/components/members/account-workspace.tsx` places **Account** at the top of the desktop sidebar and the formatted phone above **Log out** near the bottom. It already has a named section navigation landmark, a narrow-screen section disclosure, and shared workspace links.
- `next-js/src/app/_components/account-route.tsx` resolves the session, localized section, safe return destination, and relevant data. It supplies section headings and the existing subscription checkout or payment-report action. Inspect `next-js/src/components/members/account-panels.tsx` and `next-js/src/domain/account-navigation.ts` for the shared summaries and section links. Follow the existing checkout and readiness logic in `next-js/src/app/_components/subscribe-route.tsx` and `next-js/src/server/members/sales-readiness.ts`.
- `next-js/src/components/members/account-records.tsx` already uses the admin record layout for member lists and details. Start from it and `next-js/src/components/admin/record-workspace.tsx` for selection, pane geometry, detail actions, and mobile Back behavior. Inspect `account-notifications.tsx`, `account-activity.tsx`, `account-payments.tsx`, `account-wallet.tsx`, `account-referrals.tsx`, and `account-security.tsx` in the member component directory before changing their sections.

## Shared layout and identity

- Display the registered phone number, formatted with the existing phone formatter, in the desktop sidebar slot currently labeled **Account**. Use it as the visible identity and keep a meaningful accessible name for the navigation landmark. Remove the phone text above **Log out** and the old identity block and excess spacing. Bring **Log out** closer to the section list. Give it the available row width and center its icon and label together within the control. Retain the established sign-out behavior.
- Adapt the phone identity to the visible mobile Account header while keeping the current-section label and disclosure usable. Keep the identity in one workspace chrome location per viewport. Keep a distinct phone fact on Overview as requested. The disclosure and sign-out action must remain reachable on narrow and short screens.
- Remove repeated visible section titles and repeated facts within each pane. Retain headings that describe distinct groups or a selected record, the programmatic page or section heading and IDs used to associate controls and content, navigation landmarks, and accessible control names. Keep actionable errors, labels, progress, statuses and confirmations available. A shared fact can appear separately in its own section or record detail when it serves a distinct purpose.

## Section requirements

- **Overview.** Group the useful account, subscription, referral and security facts into compact, readable sections. Show the registered phone once in the main content, in addition to its identity display in the sidebar or mobile header. Keep meaningful actions beside the related facts. Remove overlapping action labels and competing links that lead to the same work.
- **Subscription.** Present the plan, its current status, and the relevant expiry or renewal date once. Keep distinctions for an active trial, past expiry, queued access and a genuine pending payment when the data supports them. Offer **Subscribe**, **Renew**, or **Extend** where the member’s current state and eligibility support the action. Continue through the existing checkout and preserve the safe return destination. Keep **View payment** or equivalent recovery for an existing report where applicable. Preserve paused or unavailable sales behavior and existing access rules.
- **Wallet, Referrals and Payments.** Use compact summaries, concise rows and a selected detail pane for useful record lists, following the existing Account patterns. Preserve balances, credit and top-up rules, wallet renewal settings, reward eligibility and redemption, reviews or appeals, payment evidence and status workflows, and the actions, history and pagination each section already supports. Keep existing search or filter scope accurate. Put record actions with the selected detail when appropriate and keep section-wide actions discoverable.
- **Phone and Password.** Use a balanced form width, field spacing, and action alignment on desktop. Stack controls comfortably on mobile. Keep the current phone as a clear fact where useful. Preserve field labels, autocomplete, validation, password and recovery checks, session behavior, secure return and cancel behavior, and visible pending or failure feedback. Avoid repeated panel titles that merely restate the section.
- **Devices.** Present a compact signed-in device list, distinguish the current device, and keep useful existing activity information. Keep the existing actions and their permissions, including signing out other devices. Preserve feedback, confirmation where present, and empty and unavailable states.
- **Notifications.** Keep this section. Review what the inbox actually delivers and how its unread, read and dismiss actions, linked records, and pagination differ from Activity and the other sections. Record any concrete overlap and which user job it serves for a later decision. Preserve inbox notices, unread indicators, actions, links and pagination now. Use compact rows and a selected detail pane on desktop, with the existing list-to-detail and Back flow on mobile.
- **Activity.** Use the admin-style compact list and adjacent selected detail on desktop. On mobile, open a detail view and restore the prior list context on Back. Use the existing member-visible event types and fields in `next-js/src/components/members/account-activity.tsx` and `next-js/src/server/members/account-dashboard.ts`. Preserve account ownership, privacy boundaries, selection and available history or paging. Keep the current bounded feed accurate. Add controls only where the existing data and workflow make them useful.

## Repository standards and acceptance

- Make web changes in `next-js/`. Reuse shared controls, row heights, spacing and theme tokens. Use subtle corners from `theme.radius` and follow `next-js/docs/corner-radius.md` and `next-js/docs/control-uniformity.md`. Keep unchanged header, navigation and workspace chrome mounted. Use shared `NavigationProgress` immediately below the app header for foreground work. Mark pending content with `aria-busy` or use `AppLoading` when there is no content container. Keep existing controls and labels visible where possible and passive refresh quiet.
- Verify desktop and mobile in light and dark themes, including narrow and short viewports, long formatted identities, keyboard operation, visible focus, disclosure dismissal and the centered sign-out control. Confirm the phone in the sidebar or mobile header and as a distinct Overview fact. Confirm sign-out takes the member to the established destination and removes private content and identity. Check that guests, restricted sessions and administrators retain existing route handling.
- Verify each section’s distinct visible headings and facts, selected states, localized deep links and approved return destinations. Open a Notification and an Activity record, use mobile Back and browser Back and Forward, and check list selection, focus and scroll restoration where applicable. Confirm existing pagination and detail actions and check populated, empty, pending and unavailable states.
- Follow the actual subscription action from Account into an eligible checkout. Confirm the available plan or payment step loads and works through its normal progression with test data. Exercise an active renewal or extension, an inactive member starting a subscription, a pre-existing payment report and paused or unavailable checkout. Verify safe return navigation. Use local or approved sandbox fixtures for payment progression and preserve sales gates.
- Exercise phone and password form validation, successful or restricted outcomes, and pending/failure feedback. Exercise device sign-out with current and other device states. Run the relevant Account navigation, member, notifications and checkout checks, and regression-check the admin record layout if shared primitives change.
- Update `APP_OVERVIEW.md` with the actual behavior during the future implementation. Distinguish verified source changes from hosted deployment status.
