Mobile search and filters, ten ways

Ten designs for the search bar and filter area on phones. Each shows the screen at rest and the moment a filter is open. All ten keep the brand's square corners, market colours and labels. Pick one by number and I will build it.

Every design includes

  • Search
  • Match dates
  • Kickoff time
  • Markets
  • Leagues
  • Countries
  • Chance of the pick
  • Odds (unavailable)
  • Sort
  • Clear and Apply
  • Match count

My pick

1 · Day Strip. Switching days is the most common action, so it takes one tap. Every other filter sits in one predictable sheet with one Apply. Runner-up: 5 · Filter List for the clearest overview.

Days are one tap away in a strip. Every other filter waits in one sheet.
