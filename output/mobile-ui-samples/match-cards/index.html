Mobile match cards, ten ways

Ten compact designs for the match card on phones. Each design shows the same 14 matches: today's list in light mode and yesterday's results in dark mode, so you can see every state. All ten keep the brand's square corners and market colours. Pick one by number and I will build it.

Every card shows

  • League and flag
  • Kickoff time
  • Live minute, HT, FT
  • Postponed and other states
  • Teams and crests
  • Score
  • Market
  • Pick
  • Chance
  • Odds, when imported
  • Won, Lost or Void
  • Why there is no pick
  • Notes such as limited news

Shared rules

  • One day header carries the date and time zone, so cards show only the time.
  • Won, Lost or Void appears only once the pick is settled.
  • A settled score is the 90-minute score; AET or Pens says more was played.
  • The whole card opens the match details.
